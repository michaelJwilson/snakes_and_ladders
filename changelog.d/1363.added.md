Annealing ends with a polisher run to its own convergence: `anneal_potts(..., polish=Polish.ICM)` descends from the final state until a full ICM sweep changes no label (`Stop.CONVERGED`), and HMC `anneal(..., polish=polish_by_fit, polish_budget=Budget(Cost.ITERATIONS, n))` runs L-BFGS to its gradient tolerance (`Stop.BUDGET` at its cap). `Annealed` gains `polish_spent` and `polished_by`; `polish=None` is the unpolished run, bitwise.
