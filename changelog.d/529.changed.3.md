The textbook's reinforcement-learning sections now state the methods the
package carries and the ones that apply beside them: the two policy
parameterizations and the gauge they share, exploration as a declared
epsilon-greedy behaviour policy on a schedule with the restart-matched
baseline it entails, the state features a critic reads and the rule that
keeps its baseline unbiased, off-policy correction with its support condition
and the variance a ratio costs, compound moves, planning on a learned model,
and a subsection on relaxing a discrete decision --- corner exactness,
multilinearity as its boundary, and the bias a reparameterized categorical
sample carries. Each of the eleven problem statements ends with what it would
take to go further and what it deliberately does not attempt, and a closing
subsection states the three directions that recur across them.
