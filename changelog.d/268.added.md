`snakes_and_ladders.opt.fit.standard_errors_at` gives a fit an interval from the observed information at that point, whatever optimizer produced it, and `fit(include_intervals=True)` / `fit_from(include_intervals=True)` attach one to the fit they return. Every objective now implements `theta_from`, the inverse of its constraint map, so an expectation-maximization fit — which never builds an unconstrained vector — can be given the same interval a gradient fit gets. The refusals are unchanged and gain one: a point where the information is singular, indefinite or ill-conditioned is still refused, and so is an unconverged fit, rather than either being given a meaningless interval.
