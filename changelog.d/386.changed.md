exchange step in `opt.anneal`. `drive` runs the loop `search.potts_mcmc`,
`search.gibbs` (twice) and `opt.hmc` each carried; `exchange` runs the pass
over adjacent replicas the two `parallel_tempering` functions and
`search.tempered` each carried, on the one `swap_log_ratio` that had been
written three times. `learn.relaxed.anneal` becomes `opt.schedule.Exponential`
under its own bounds. Every entry point keeps its signature and is equal to
the loop it replaced draw for draw; the relaxed schedule is within 4.2e-16 of
its old expression with both endpoints exact.
