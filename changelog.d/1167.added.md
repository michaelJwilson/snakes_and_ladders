`sal.opt.hmm.forward_log_likelihood_ragged(log_density, lengths, log_initial, log_transition)` (#1167): the differentiable forward log-likelihood over segments of unequal length, in `Ragged.values` layout, on the shared `forward_messages` recursion. It equals `forward_log_likelihood_from_density` bitwise on equal lengths and the summed evidence of `sal.likelihood.ragged.posteriors` within 1e-12; its gradient with respect to the scores is the posterior marginal (Fisher identity).
