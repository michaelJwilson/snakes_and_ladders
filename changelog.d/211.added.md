`phylo.learn.relaxed` implements the Gumbel-softmax half of `ROADMAP.md` Stage 3's differentiable-search bullet: continuous relaxations of Potts configurations and HMM state paths, whose optima are enumerable and can therefore referee the claim. The relaxation reduces to the discrete objective exactly at every corner, and `E_q[score] = score(q)` holds for any multilinear objective — which licenses a deterministic ascent with no sampling at all. Both Gumbel-softmax estimators are measured against the exact gradient rather than assumed accurate: over 20000 draws the bias falls from 0.598 at `tau = 2.0` to 0.036 at `tau = 0.1` while the standard deviation rises from 0.165 to 3.39.
