`sal.external` gains one explicit call per algorithm beside its solver-keyed calls (#1304): `potts.ground_state.expansion`, `.swap` (`by=Framework.GCO` or `Framework.OPENGM`), `.min_cut`, `.icm`, `.loopy_bp`, `.astar`; `potts.lower_bound.lp`, `.trws`, `.dual_decomposition`; `hmm.fit.baum_welch`. The solver-keyed calls keep their signatures and `match` onto these, returning equal results for all 12 solvers they cover. A keyword the explicit call does not take, such as `tolerance` for HiGHS, is refused before any subprocess starts. `sal.external.frameworks.Framework` is now the enum of framework names; the registry entry is `Registration`.
