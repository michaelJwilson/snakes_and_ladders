OpenGM as a validation referee (#1279): `infra/build_opengm.sh` builds its header-only inference against a pinned commit into `~/.cache/sal/opengm` (16 s, 29 MB, no Boost or HDF5), and `sal.external.potts` routes seven new solvers to it: `ground_state` for ICM, loopy BP, A*, alpha expansion and alpha-beta swap, `lower_bound` for TRW-S and dual decomposition, with forbidden labels, sessions and `max_iterations`/`tolerance` on `lower_bound`. A* equals enumeration to 1e-12; TRW-S's limit equals `search.trws`'s to 1e-8 where both reach the LP; ICM equals `search.icm` bitwise. OpenGM's expansion and swap carry upstream defects, pinned as `bug` tests.
