Felsenstein pruning under PyTorch builds a leaf's message by gathering the
transition matrix's rows at the observed states, rather than materializing a
one-hot matrix and multiplying it; the seed for an internal node's product is
its first child's message rather than a tensor of ones. Both are bitwise the
arithmetic they replace. Measured on the eight-taxon NNI hill climb at 300
evaluations, one BLAS thread: 26.2 s to 22.9 s cold, 18.3 s to 14.6 s warm,
2.0 s to 1.7 s lazy.
