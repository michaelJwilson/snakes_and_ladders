Felsenstein pruning under PyTorch builds a leaf's message by gathering the
transition matrix's rows at the observed states, rather than materializing a
one-hot matrix and multiplying it; the seed for an internal node's product is
its first child's message rather than a tensor of ones; and the rescaling
factor is detached, since it cancels between the division it performs and the
log it is added to and so carries no derivative. The log-likelihood is
unchanged bitwise. Measured on the eight-taxon NNI hill climb at 300
evaluations, one BLAS thread: 26.2 s to 17.4 s cold, 18.3 s to 11.1 s warm,
2.0 s to 1.4 s lazy.
