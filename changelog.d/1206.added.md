A JAX twin of `EmissionHmmObjective` for count pairs on segments of unequal length (#1206): `CountPairEmission` (joint and independent), `RateConcentrationCountPairEmission`, `sim.count_pairs.IndependentCountPair` and `RateConcentrationBetaBinomialEmission` take `backend=Backend.JAX`, with the per-channel covariate; unequal segments are padded to the longest and masked, so one jitted program serves them.
