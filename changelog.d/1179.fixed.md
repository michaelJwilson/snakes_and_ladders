`sal.opt.em.em_loop` raises `ValueError` at the iteration whose log-likelihood is `nan` or `±inf`, naming it, where a non-finite value failed every relative test and ran out the EM budget as `Stop.BUDGET` (#1179). Every EM entry point loops through it: `baum_welch`, `baum_welch_family` and both mixture `expectation_maximization`s, on the torch and streamed Rust routes. Finite fits are bitwise unchanged.
