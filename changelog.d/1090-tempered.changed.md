The three temperings that return a best point return one `sal.sample.schedule.Tempered` each: `parallel_tempering` (Potts), `cluster_tempering` and the HMC `parallel_tempering`. The best point is `best` (was `theta` on the HMC result), its value `energy` (was `best_energy`) or `value`, the cost `spent` in a declared `unit` (was `site_visits`, `force_evaluations`, or `n_sweeps` to multiply by the replicas), and each carries `temperatures`, `swap_acceptance` (a NumPy array on the HMC result, which was a tensor) and a `termination` (#1090).
