`likelihood.ragged.sample_paths(log_density, log_initial, log_transition, rng, *, switch=None, switch_kind=..., backend=Backend.RUST) -> SampledPaths(path, log_joint, log_evidence)` (#1170): one posterior draw of every segment's hidden path, forward filter and backward sample, under every `SwitchKind`. The uniforms are `rng.random(total)`, drawn in Python; `oxisal.ragged_sample_paths` and the oracle `sample_paths_oracle` invert one CDF rule (`forward_backward.inverse_cdf`) and return the same path. Over 2e5 positions at ten states the compiled route takes 158 ms against the oracle's 2.66 s (16.9x), 181 ms against 2.94 s with a stay-or-move switch (16.2x), and 84 ms against 5.46 s under `KRONECKER` (64.7x). `ragged.sampled_posteriors(..., rng, *, n_paths=4)` averages `n_paths` draws into a Monte-Carlo `Posteriors` with the exact evidence. `baum_welch_family(..., *, e_step=None)` takes an `opt.hmm.EStep`, any `(log_density: Ragged, log_initial, log_transition) -> Posteriors`; `None` is the existing route, and `ragged.posteriors` as the hook reproduces it bitwise.
