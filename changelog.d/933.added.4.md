Mixture EM takes a per-observation covariate: `expectation_maximization`, `anneal_assignments`, `mixture_log_likelihood`, `responsibilities` and `e_step` score and re-estimate under it, and `MixtureInstance` carries one to every start. `sim.count_pairs.rate_space` and `IndependentCountPairSeeding` seed `IndependentCountPair` components at rates rather than raw counts (R1).
