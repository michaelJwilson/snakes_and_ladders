`learn`: the score-function loop is `rollout.log_probabilities_of`, written once and read by REINFORCE, the actor-critic, PPO's ratio and entropy, and expert iteration's cross-entropy; the four episode tails are `Episode.from_rollout`, which reads `terminated` from the state a walk ended in; the critic's batch is `critic.state_targets` and its values `critic.state_values`; and `PlannedEpisode` is an `Episode`, so expert iteration reads `returns_to_go` rather than recomputing it. Every estimator, every rollout and the training curve bitwise at a fixed seed (#862).

`sim`: the two mixtures share `mixture.MixtureParamsBase`, generic over its component family, and `mixture.draw_mixture`; each keeps its own params type, entry point and `Dataset`, so a rung's declared output is unchanged. The two `BinInstance` classes reading one yaml block are `fixtures.BinInstance`, carrying what a bin holds --- a draw or a position --- as a field. Both lattice builders run one `_lattice` over their own offsets. The five fixture loaders that read a boundary go through `graph.boundary_from_declared`, so a misspelt one names the file and the recognized values at all five rather than at two. The count-pair binning's divisibility check is `_bin`, written once. Every fixture draw is bitwise what it was; the count-pair fixture's record gains the bin's unit and nothing else (#862).
