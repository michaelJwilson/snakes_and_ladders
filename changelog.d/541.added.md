`snakes_and_ladders.search.projection` compares eight seedings of the coupled
model's emission parameters at an equal fit budget, with cost counted in passes
over the data, and `opt.initialize` gains `FromChain`, `FromAnnealing` and
`FromTempering`, which start a fit from a chain on a surrogate surface. No
default seeding changed: at the key model the projected likelihood and the
simulated truth order the candidates oppositely
(`docs/experiments/009-projection-emission-seedings.md`, #559).
