`infra/appraise_structures.py` derives the package's data-structure inventory
instead of recalling it: every class that carries state, its fields, its
public methods, the modules importing it, and the clusters of classes sharing
a shape. The two surveys it joins — `infra/duplication_survey.py` and
`infra/seams_survey.py` — carry hand-written lists, so they re-count what is
known and discover nothing, and `infra/gate_new_seams.py` exists to enforce
that a hand list gets updated, which is the work the list was meant to save.

**201 state-carrying classes, 7 clusters at three or more members.** The one
the ticket rests on is `role:incidence` at **11 members and 71 consuming
references**: one relation stored three ways. `sim.ldpc.ParityCheck` and
`search.gibbs._EdgeLayout` carry CSR; `sim.graph.PottsGraph` carries COO and
derives CSR on **every call**; eight more are object graphs.

Two runtime findings came out of the walk rather than out of a reader, each
naming the rule it breaks in root `CLAUDE.md`'s Runtime Optimization
Opportunities: `search.maxflow.FlowNetwork` carries a list of lists where the
rule asks for offsets into one array, and `sim.graph.PottsGraph`'s
`compressed_adjacency` rebuilds a compressed layout per call from a store that
is not compressed.
