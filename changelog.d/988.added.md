`hmc.sample` gains `store_chain` and `operators` (#988). With `store_chain=False` the chain keeps no draws, and each named operator's expectation over the recorded draws comes back in `HmcChain.expectations`, estimated by `sample.expectation.KalmanMean`: a Kalman filter for a constant mean observed through AR(1) noise, held as six running sums per coordinate. Its estimate is the conditional GLS mean within 1e-12, and its 95 per cent intervals cover at 0.9525 on 400 AR(1) streams at phi = 0.9, where a white-noise interval covers 0.34. At d = 10^4 and 1,000 transitions the chain's peak added memory falls from 110 MB to 35 MB. The defaults leave every chain bitwise as it was.
