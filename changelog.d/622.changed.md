Five test modules load their declared instance from
`snakes_and_ladders.sim.fixtures` instead of rebuilding it from literals, so
`-m <problem>` selects them: the five-component mixture budget, the count-HMM
and turbo benchmarks, the Potts heat-bath conditional and the max-flow
benchmark. Each conversion is bitwise the instance it replaced --- a
redirection, not a re-parameterization. Step 2 of issue #622.
