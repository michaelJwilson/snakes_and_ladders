`sal.emissions.coded` scores the count emissions on coded observations
(issue #1340, stage 1): `encode` codes integer counts per distinct value in
Rust per `(label, count)`, `label` one integer array the caller builds, giving an `int32` inverse, a weight per code and the covariate per
observation, and `log_emission` builds the tables at the distinct counts
only, completes the covariate term per observation and gathers, bitwise
equal to `emissions.dense.log_emission` on the same observations.

Stage 2: `emissions.coded.log_emission_sum(family, coded, weights=None)`, a
per-state `bincount` over the inverse in Rust, and
`log_emission_partials(family, coded)`, each parameter's partial through
`digamma_rising`, checked against mpmath and central differences.
