Yoshida's fourth-order symplectic integrator, selectable in `snakes_and_ladders.opt.hmc.sample` beside leapfrog. Both are compositions of the same kick-drift-kick sub-step, and `Integrator.force_evaluations` reports what a trajectory costs so the two can be compared at equal gradient evaluations rather than at equal steps. Leapfrog remains the default: it reaches the same acceptance for a quarter of the gradients on the objectives measured.
