New `opt.split_merge`: split-and-merge EM for a mixture of emissions (Ueda, Nakano, Ghahramani & Hinton, 2000). `split_and_merge(fit, observations, at, *, candidates)` ranks merge pairs by the cosine of their responsibility columns (`merge_criterion`) and split candidates by the local Kullback-Leibler divergence (`split_criterion`), realizes each move as a re-weighting of the responsibilities that the family's own M step fits, runs partial EM on the three components and full EM, and keeps a move only if the log-likelihood rises; every candidate is a `SplitMergeStep`. On `emission_mixture/ci`, from a fixed point with one component across two generating ones and two identical ones on the third, the first move gains 165.7 nats and reaches EM's maximum from the truth.
