`opt.emission_mixture.seed(observations, n_components, at, *, method, rng, rows=None)` seeds a mixture from the observations alone, with no known truth, by `uniform`, `plus_plus` or `kmeans`, and returns a `Start` (weights and components) that `expectation_maximization(observations, *start)` takes as it is. `search.mixture_starts`' data, emission and k-means seedings call it (#1085).
