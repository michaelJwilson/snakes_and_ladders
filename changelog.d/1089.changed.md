Three conventions that could give a wrong answer silently now have one reading each (#1089). **Breaking:** the `learn` environments' maximized log-density is `log_weight` everywhere: `PottsEnvironment.energy` and `HmmEnvironment.energy` (both maximized, under the name the package minimizes) and `PottsNdEnvironment.score` and `TreeEnvironment.score` are renamed, so `energy` always means the minimized `-log_weight`. **Breaking:** the annealers (`annealed_importance_sampling`, `population_annealing`, `simulated_tempering`) take a `TempSchedule` or the new `sample.schedule.InverseTemperatures`, and refuse a bare sequence, which they read as inverse temperatures where tempering reads temperatures; `geometric_betas` returns `InverseTemperatures`. An exhausted loop is a `Termination`: alpha-expansion and the swap no longer warn at their cycle cap, and `opt.termination.check_cap` refuses a cap below 1 alike in `trws` and `dual_bound`, which returned a bound of `-inf`; message passing's `ConvergenceError` stays, and says why.
