An `m_step` hook on `baum_welch_family` and `emission_mixture.expectation_maximization` (#1171): an `opt.m_step.MStep` replaces the family's `reestimate`, and `LbfgsMStep` maximizes the expected complete-data log-likelihood by L-BFGS over the family's declared domains, then Newton steps, holding named parameters fixed; on closed-form families it reaches the closed form within 4.9e-11. `EmFit` and `EmissionMixtureFit` gain `stages`. A tempered schedule failed its gate on a multimodal Gaussian HMM (no schedule beat plain EM over ten starts) and stays in `sandbox.annealed_em`: `annealed_baum_welch` is new there, both annealers take a `TempSchedule`, and `free_energy` is renamed `log_tempered_evidence`.
