`opt.objective.Restricted(objective, at, varied)`: any `Objective` with every coordinate outside `varied` held at `at`, promoted from `search.infer`'s private `_Restricted`. It forwards `value_and_gradient`, `gradient` and `energy` through the inner objective's own routes, so a compiled gradient stays compiled inside it. `DeclaredBlocks` names an objective's `theta` blocks, declared by every HMM objective and `EmissionMixtureObjective`, and `coordinates(objective, names)` turns block names into the `varied` indices.
