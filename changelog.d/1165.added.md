`EmFit`, `CategoricalFit`, `EmissionMixtureFit` and `MixtureFit` carry `spent` in `unit` (`Cost.ITERATIONS`, the EM iterations run) beside `termination`, and `EmFit.frozen` lists the states an M step held because the E step left them no data, as `EmissionMixtureFit.frozen` does (issue #1165). `opt.em.inner_termination` states an M step's inner solve, a `Reestimate` among others, as a `Termination`.
