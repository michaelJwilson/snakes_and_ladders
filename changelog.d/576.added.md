A typeset map of the package's Python surface (`infra/api_map.py`, issue
#576): every module, class, public function and public method, each with the
first paragraph of its own docstring, laid out in package order rather than
alphabetically because the structure is part of what is being shown. It builds
`docs/api_map.pdf`, the third committed document, at **1,528 entries over 64
pages** --- 151 modules, 251 classes, 587 functions and 539 methods.

Read by `ast` and never by importing, the route `infra/select_tests.py`
already takes: no import side effect, no compiled extension, and no module
dropped because its import failed. A map that omits quietly is worse than no
map, so `tests/regression/docs/test_api_map.py` counts the entries against a
**second walk of the tree** rather than checking that the generator ran, and
asserts every module's own label reaches the rendered output.

Two properties the surface is reported against rather than trusted for: a
public top-level function with no summary line would be typeset as a blank, so
`--write` refuses one and the suite shows the refusal firing on a tree that
has one; and 41 summaries over 100 characters are listed on stderr to be
shortened where they are written, wrapped by the layout and never cut.
