`snakes_and_ladders.opt.potts.PottsLatticeObjective` fits a Potts model's coupling and external field on an arbitrary graph, against a normalizer enumerated exactly over every configuration. Interval coverage measured over 40 replicates is 0.981 at 100 samples and 0.956 at 400 and 1600, approaching the nominal 0.95 from above; the enumerated normalizer reduces to the chain's transfer matrix to machine precision.
