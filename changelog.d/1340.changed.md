`sal.oxisal.dense_log_emission` is deprecated for one release cycle in favour of `sal.emissions.coded.log_emission(family, Dense(counts, covariate))` (#1340, stage 3). It keeps its #1348 signature, `log_rate` included, as a thin binding over the kernel `coded_log_emission` reads, and its output is bitwise unchanged on six saved cases. `sal.emissions.dense.log_emission` and `sal.emissions.dense.Order` are removed; `Order` has had no effect since #1336, and no caller outside the tests used either. **Breaking:** `from sal.emissions.dense import log_emission` or `Order` fails.
