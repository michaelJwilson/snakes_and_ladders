The `documents` CI job now runs on a pull request that changes a document
input --- `docs/tex/`, `PROBLEMS.md`, `infra/problems_tables.py`,
`infra/build_documents.sh` or `python/snakes_and_ladders/qa/` --- and typesets
both documents without regenerating the figures. A document that no longer
builds is caught where it is cheap rather than after the merge (issue #625).
