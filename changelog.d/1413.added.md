`sal.qa.potts_labelling`: a downstream labelling step's solvers --- field argmax, ICM with and without the floor, alpha-expansion+ICM, TRW-S, ICM+merge and the anneal with `Polish.ICM_MERGE` --- from one field and start on `potts_labelling/{ci,stress}`, a `potts_reference` cell mirroring the downstream stream's problems (3,000 sites, q = 5, degree 6, floor 50), with `easy`, `hard` and `dev_6000` variants spanning them. The cell gains `margin_spread` (a log-normal per-site margin) and `frequency` (the plane waves' wavenumber factor), both bitwise off by default. `docs/experiments/040-*.md` reports each arm's gap to the TRW-S bound, labels, wall, peak and Rust crossings, and the four causes that separate the downstream path from sal's.
