`hmc.anneal` and `hmc.parallel_tempering` take the compiled route where the objective has `supported_gradient()`, the integrator is leapfrog and no run is tracked (issue #1249): `sal.sample.loop.anneal` and `temper` run over a step that advances an `oxisal.HmcWalk` one transition at the temperature it is handed, and an exchange hands each walk the other's position with its carried `(U, grad U)`. Both take `backend=Backend.RUST` as `hmc.sample` does; `Backend.PYTHON` keeps the torch route. The compiled draws come from the walk's ChaCha8 stream, so they differ from the torch route's at one seed and match it in distribution. Per proposal on a Gaussian at d = 10, annealing takes 1.8 µs against 124 µs and a four-rung ladder 6.1 µs against 132 µs. `anneal` with an `adaptation` stays on torch. A compiled rung after a warm-up carries `grad U` across its metric, so its rounds charge `n_steps` gradients where the torch route's charge `n_steps + 1`. `HmcWalk` and `MetropolisWalk` gain `state()`, `set_state(position, value, gradient)` and `advance_at(n, temperature)`; `sample.chain.compiled_walk` builds one. `FromAnnealing` and `FromTempering` take `backend` and pass it on.
