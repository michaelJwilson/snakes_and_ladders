`sal.emissions.dense.log_emission(family, observations, covariate, *, order)` returns a count family's log-emission, shape `(K, ...)`, from its tables through `oxisal.dense_log_emission`, for the negative binomial under an exposure, the beta-binomial under a trial count, and their independent pair (#1132). The beta-binomial is `log_density` bitwise; the negative binomial's exposure term is completed in the family's order (6.7 ulp relative) or the coupled kernel's (`Order.TABULATED`). At six states over 320,000 observations the pair takes 10.1 ms tabulated and 14.5 ms in the family's order, against 200.5 ms for `log_density`.
