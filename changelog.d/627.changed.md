Three Rust kernels take a `rayon` thread pool over their independent axis:
`pruning_log_likelihood` over sites (**3.82x** at 8 taxa and 200,000 sites),
the coupled model's `external_field` over vertices (**2.38x**), and
`sample_rows` over draws (**2.04x**). All three are bit-identical to the serial
path, so no oracle tolerance is spent. Three further proposals were measured
and declined, `class_posteriors` among them at 0.86x (issue #627).
