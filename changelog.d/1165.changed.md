`baum_welch_family` and `baum_welch` take `covariate` and `backend` as keyword-only arguments, and `opt.mixture.expectation_maximization` takes `backend` as keyword-only, matching `opt.emission_mixture.expectation_maximization` (issue #1165). A positional call raises `TypeError`; pass them by name.
`CategoricalFit` unpacks to its seven fields in declared order, `spent` and `unit` after `termination` (issue #865); a caller unpacking the five-tuple takes the rest with `*_`.
