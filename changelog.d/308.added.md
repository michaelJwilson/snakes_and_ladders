Surrogates for the expensive evaluations, each carrying its claim: certified analytic bounds (plug-in and parsimony bounds on a topology's maximized log-likelihood; mean-field and spanning-tree bounds on a lattice's `log Z`, and the ground-state bracket they give), learned predictors and calibrated bounds on the bound features (`sal.learn.surrogate`), and `infer(..., surrogate=)` to rank a neighbourhood by any of them before fitting the top candidates.
