`opt.emission_mixture.expectation_maximization` takes `temperatures`: deterministic annealing EM (Ueda & Nakano, 1998), one E and one M step at each temperature with responsibilities `softmax((log w + log p) / T)`, then the tolerance loop at one; `None` and `[1.0]` are the plain fit bitwise. `free_energy` gives the value a tempered step ascends, and `EmissionMixtureFit` carries each step's `temperatures` and `free_energies`. `opt.em.em_loop` takes the log-likelihood before its start as `previous`, `-inf` by default. On `emission_mixture/ci` the annealed and plain fits reach one maximum from every `data` seed tried.
