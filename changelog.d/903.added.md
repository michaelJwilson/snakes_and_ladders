`sandbox.annealed_em` conserves deterministic annealing EM for the emission mixture (Ueda & Nakano, 1998), declined (#916): `annealed_expectation_maximization` runs one E and one M step at each temperature with responsibilities `softmax((log w + log p) / T)`, then plain EM at one, and reproduces `opt.emission_mixture.expectation_maximization` bitwise at an empty schedule and at `[1.0]`; `free_energy` gives the value a tempered step ascends. On `emission_mixture/ci` annealed and plain EM reach the same maximum from every `data` seed tried, so `opt` keeps its signature. `opt.em.em_loop` takes the log-likelihood before its start as `previous`, `-inf` by default.
