`wolff_sweep` and `niedermayer_sweep` take the adjacency as lists built once per chain (`sample.potts_mcmc.adjacency_lists`) rather than converting it on every step: a Wolff step on a 50x50 lattice at q = 3 takes 20.7 µs rather than 314 µs at β = 0.5, bitwise the same stream. The cluster moves' exactness is now pinned in a per-site field as well as a shared one, and the coupled model's negation of its field when it hands it to a cluster move is pinned. The textbook states the two field conventions and the β → 0 limit of the move.
