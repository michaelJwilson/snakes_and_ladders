`sal.search.trws`: a lower bound on the Potts ground-state energy from the local-polytope relaxation, by sequential tree-reweighted message passing (TRW-S), with a `numba` kernel pinned bitwise to its Python reference (issue #1060). On `spatio_only/release` (5,041 sites, 10 states) the bound meets the graph-cut optimum, -10,454.16, in 43 iterations and 0.23 s, and certifies it. On `spatio_tiling/release` it converges to -17,022.98 in 1,825 iterations, 0.81 below the lowest labelling found; the factor-2 bracket there was 293.3 wide. Enumeration, `search.tightening.dual_bound` at convergence and the tree case referee it; the explicit LP through HiGHS is issue #1063.
