`search.mixture_starts.best_of(name, n)` runs a stochastic start `n` times, each seeding on its own generator spawned from the cell's, and hands over the best seeding at `n` times the cost. `best_of(..., Selection.POLISHED)` instead polishes every seeding by EM and keeps the best fit. `workers` and `pool` run the seedings through `snakes_and_ladders.parallel`, bitwise equal to one worker. The starts notebook adds both best-of-5 groups, and it and `potts_starts` have a 1,000 s notebook budget where the rest keep 600 s.
