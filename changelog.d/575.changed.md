The textbook is restructured on the maintainer's review of the draft: the
problems are stated first, ordered by complexity, and the algorithms follow in
a part of their own, so an algorithm serving several problems is stated once
and specialized per problem. Reinforcement learning, Hamiltonian Monte Carlo,
tempering and interval estimates become self-contained appendices; the
notation becomes one captioned table splitting general symbols from
application ones; the validation ladder and its four tiers are stated as a
ladder; and neighbor joining, the Hadamard spectrum, spectral learning and the
transfer matrix each get a section rather than a paragraph. Two figures are
added, the NNI and SPR move sets over successive steps and the four-point
condition, and the fixture tree is drawn at eight taxa with its branch lengths
and leaf sequences.
