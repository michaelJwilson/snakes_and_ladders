`sample.hmc.parallel_tempering` takes `adaptation`, a per-rung dual-averaging warm-up of step and mass before the rounds, and `start` as one point per rung (issue #1208). `sample.hmc.anneal` takes `adaptation`, re-tuning the step by dual averaging every `adaptation.warmup` proposals along the schedule, a heuristic. Without `adaptation` both are bitwise unchanged.
`search.hmm_starts` adds `chain_adapted`, `annealed_adapted` and `tempered_adapted` to `STARTS`: at equal passes they reach the truth's basin in 3, 0 and 3 of 10 seeds on four states against the grid-tuned steps' 10, 10 and 10, so `STEP` and `ANNEALING_STEP` stay. `sample.chain`'s `warm_up`, `Scaled`, `DualAveraging` and `jittered` are public, since `sample.hmc` now calls them.
