The `critical` marker now admits a fast check of the science, not only a test
that is structurally prior: *"its failure means the rest is not worth running
--- structurally, or because the science it rests on is wrong"*. Measured under
the older reading, the tier was 149 infrastructure tests of 177 and covered
36.80% of the package. Built out against that reading, it is **298 tests
covering 48.27%**, 5.9 s more of the 4-core host's clock, with
`likelihood/message_passing.py` at 94% and `search/topology.py` at 89% where
both were near 20%. `tests/regression/test_critical_coverage.py` holds the tier
to the new floor, raised as tests land and never lowered, and a test that gates
early while a fixture declares it `release`, `stress` or `key` is now refused at
collection (issue #635).
