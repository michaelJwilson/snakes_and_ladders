`parallel_tempering` takes one `PottsMove` per rung, or per rung a tuple of moves run in order each step, and `rung_moves` chooses them from each rung's `beta J` against the critical coupling: heat-bath Swendsen-Wang alone below it, heat-bath Swendsen-Wang then a single-site sweep at and above it (#1158). `ground_state.METHODS` gains `tempering-mixed`, which reaches -10,215.9 against `tempering`'s -9,900.1 on `spatio_only/release` at equal site visits.
