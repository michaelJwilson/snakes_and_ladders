`Polish.ICM_MERGE`: ICM from `best`, then the greedy whole-label merge until no merge lowers the energy, in the Rust loop's one call, with the NumPy `merge_labels` as its oracle. `AnnealedPotts.stages` and `AnnealedTheta.stages` report each stage run (`init`, `polish`, `merge`) as a `Stage`: its point, energy, spent, wall seconds and termination.
