`infra/gates.py` states the gate policy in one place: the three gates and what each selects, the two duration caps, the review table's rows and budget, and the nine registered markers. A `structural` test reads every copy of those values from its own source text — `pyproject.toml`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `infra/validate.sh`, `infra/review_gates.sh`, `infra/release.sh`, `infra/test_kinds.py`, `DEV.md` — and fails when one drifts. The module is descriptive: nothing reads it for behaviour.
