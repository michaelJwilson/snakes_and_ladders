`sal.emissions.bb.trial_tables` builds the beta-binomial from scaled rising factorials and log rates with no Beta-function constant, so the dense, coupled and spatio_sequential kernels stay within 1e-11 nats of `mpmath` up to a concentration of 1e16, where the `lgamma` difference lost 5e-3 nats at 1e12 (issue #1332). The kernels' `log_beta` argument is replaced by `log_rate`.
