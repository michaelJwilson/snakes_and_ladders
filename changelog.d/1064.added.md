The Rust coupled E step takes a continuous covariate (issue #1064). The negative binomial's exposure is factored out of the table: the total's table is `B = NegativeBinomialEmission.count_log_factor + r ln r + y ln mu` by count, and the kernel adds `y ln c - (y + r) ln t` per observation, one logarithm per score: 262.9 ulp from the family's `log_density` (512 declared), chosen over the family's order (2.3 ulp) for a stress E step of 1.4-1.6 s against 2.4 s in the kernel. At the ci instance of the new `spatio_sequential_counts_covariate` fixture (64,000 log-normal exposures) one E step is 0.145 s at +5 MiB, against 58.5 s and a 9.3 GB peak for the table by count and distinct exposure; at its stress instance (5,041 vertices, 2,000 positions), one E step is 5.3 s against the NumPy oracle's 28.7 s and one field 13.1 s against 364.8 s, where that table would be 2.9e13 bytes. The beta-binomial's integer trial count takes one of three exact layouts, `covariate_rows`, bitwise to one another and to the family's `log_density` per score (0 ulp over 6.4e6 scores at ci): `range` (the default) and `distinct` tabulate every `(successes, trial count)` pair, `distinct` coding the trial counts in first-appearance order with the new `oxisal.factorize` (0.16 s against `np.unique`'s 1.39 s at stress); `factored` tabulates the density's nine `lgamma` terms by their own integers (`emissions.bb`, 0.35 MB against 13.1 MB for `range`) and the kernel sums them per score in the family's order. The rows name no parameter, so `fit_spatio_sequential` builds them once (`observation_rows`) and every E step reuses them, bitwise. At stress, rows cached, one E step is 0.80 s factored, 0.84 s range and 0.84 s distinct, and one field 2.30, 2.01 and 2.03 s (medians of 15); in a one-block fit the E steps and fields take 11.3, 10.8 and 11.0 s cached and 12.5, 13.8 and 15.5 s with rows rebuilt per call, so `range` is the default and `factored` is kept for per-call use and memory. `covariate_tolerance` is removed from the public entries: the exposure is factored and the trial count is exact, and `grid_scale` refuses an integer covariate. The NumPy backend refuses a layout other than the default. `NegativeBinomialEmission.count_log_factor` moved to `emissions.nb.count_log_factor`.
