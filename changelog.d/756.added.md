Gradient-informed proposals for the discrete states: `PottsMove.LOCALLY_BALANCED` and `PottsMove.GIBBS_WITH_GRADIENTS` on the Potts lattice, `GibbsMove.LOCALLY_BALANCED` and `GibbsMove.GIBBS_WITH_GRADIENTS` over any factor graph, on one kernel in `snakes_and_ladders.search.balanced`. A proposal weights every single-variable change by `sqrt(pi(s') / pi(s))` (Zanella 2020), or by the same function of the first-order Taylor estimate of that ratio at the one-hot state (Grathwohl et al. 2021), and carries the Metropolis-Hastings correction that leaves the exact Boltzmann law invariant — pinned by chi-square against enumeration on the 2x2 and 3x3 lattices and on the frustrated triangular instance both cluster moves refuse. On these energies the estimate is the ratio, since the relaxed log weight is affine in each variable's row, and that is pinned at `1e-12` against `sim.potts.heat_bath_log_weights`, against the tape and against the enumerated energy rather than assumed.

Cluster moves for a coupling of either sign: `PottsMove.NIEDERMAYER` on the Potts lattice, which activates a bond on its energy relative to a threshold `E_0` (Niedermayer 1988) and so runs on the frustrated antiferromagnet both Fortuin-Kasteleyn moves refuse, reducing to Wolff bitwise on a ferromagnet and to a single-site Metropolis flip at `E_0 = 0`; and Houdayer's isoenergetic move (2001) on two replicas at one temperature, through `sample_potts_pair` and `search.tempered.tempered_potts_pair`. Both are pinned by chi-square against enumeration on the 3x3 open square and the 3x3 periodic triangular antiferromagnet, with the accept step and Houdayer's component each ablated and rejected. `MoveKind.NIEDERMAYER` is a selectable arm of `PottsNDEnvironment`, and `TemperedEnsemble` gains the walker trace and `search.tempered.round_trips` that a round-trip time is read from.

`log Z` by annealing, in `snakes_and_ladders.search.annealed`: `annealed_importance_sampling` (Neal 2001) and `population_annealing` (Hukushima & Iba 2003; Machta 2010) estimate the Potts normalizer on a ladder from `beta = 0`, where `log Z_0 = n log q` is exact, to the target, each returning the estimate with its standard error and the effective sample size behind it — pinned against `likelihood.potts.strip_log_partition` at three strip widths and against enumeration on the 3x3 lattices, every deviation inside three of its own standard errors, with the importance weight and the resampling each ablated. `simulated_tempering` (Marinari & Parisi 1992) samples the rung as a variable, taking those runs' per-rung `log Z` as its weights. `opt.schedule.adapt_ladder_by_round_trips` places a ladder of fixed length by the feedback criterion (Katzgraber et al. 2006) beside the acceptance-placed `adapt_ladder`, reached for the Potts lattice by `search.tempered.adapt_ladder_round_trips`; `parallel_tempering` now records the walker trace that `search.tempered.round_trips` and `up_fraction` read.
