`DEV.md` states the path from a figure request to a committed figure, in five
steps: the request names the claim, the problem class, the sizes and the
citing section; it becomes a ticket; the renderer joins the QA manifest with a
declared `seconds`; the render and the caption are committed and cited; and a
test pins every number the caption states, or the number leaves the caption.
`tests/regression/qa/test_qa_build.py` gains the guard that makes the last
step reachable: every manifest figure is cited by a document, which is the
direction `infra/check_citations.py` does not cover.
