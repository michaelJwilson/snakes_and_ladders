JAX is the default gradient of every HMM objective (issue #1000): `opt.hmm_jax` takes the value and gradient at 0.07–0.18× PyTorch autograd's runtime at 10^4–10^5 positions, pinned to autograd at 1e-10. `backend=Backend.TORCH` keeps autograd. `fit`, HMC and MALA read an objective's `value_and_gradient` where it declares one (`opt.objective.DeclaredGradient`). `jax` is a core dependency, and the `backend-jax` extra is gone. `infra/jax_decision.py` compares the two routes in fresh interpreters.
