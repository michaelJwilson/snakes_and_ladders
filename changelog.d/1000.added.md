Added a `backend-jax` extra and `Backend.JAX` (issue #1000), for benchmarking JAX against PyTorch as the autodiff backend. JAX twins of every HMM objective are in `sandbox/jax_hmm.py`. `infra/jax_decision.py` compares them with PyTorch autograd in fresh interpreters.

JAX takes 0.15–0.74× PyTorch's runtime per gradient, but its peak memory is 1.9× or more, so no twin has replaced PyTorch yet.
