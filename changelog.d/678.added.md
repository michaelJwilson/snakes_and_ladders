`infra/appraise_kernels.py`: the compiled kernels, which take the thread pool,
what crosses the boundary and what referees each one, all derived from the tree
rather than recalled. Nine Rust modules, three parallel; every kernel is pinned
against a referee its own tests import, including one that lives beside the
kernel in its adapter. `STATUS.md` carries the four measured rows, among them
the count-pair draw at its declared instance --- 30.1 s against the NumPy
oracle's 37.3 s, a ratio of 1.23x where the rule sets 2x, against 2.5x read at
the gate size.
