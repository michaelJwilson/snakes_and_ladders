`snakes_and_ladders.search.rl.TopologyEnvironment` is now `TreeEnvironment`
(issue #705). An environment is named for its problem, which is the rule #644
retired `PottsLandscape` and `StatePathLandscape` on; the problem here is
`tree`, and its fixtures are `tree_jc`, `tree_scale` and `tree_search`. The
rename moves no behaviour --- every value the tree's regression tests pin is
unchanged --- and all three retired names are now refused by a guard in
`tests/regression/test_duplication_guards.py`, since `Topology` was the third
spelling of one seam and a spelling returns unless something refuses it.
