The Potts cluster moves decide a forbidden label (a `-inf` field entry from
`sal.sim.potts.forbid`) explicitly. A recolour onto a label any cluster member
forbids is rejected, one off a forbidden label onto an allowed one is
accepted, and a heat-bath cluster that every label forbids keeps its label.
Wolff, Swendsen-Wang (both backends) and Niedermayer no longer compute
`-inf - (-inf)` and emit `RuntimeWarning`; every all-finite chain is bitwise
unchanged.
