`sal.sample.potts_mcmc` and `sal.opt.hmm` are packages, each split kernel from driver. The 2,310-line `potts_mcmc` becomes `moves` (the move sets and the negative-coupling refusal, 72 lines), `sweeps` (every sweep kernel, the cluster constructions and the Python/Rust route, 1,287) and `chains` (sampling, annealing, parallel tempering and the Houdayer pair, 908), with `__init__` at 169; the 2,245-line `hmm` becomes `forward` (the forward recursion and state alignment, 200), `objectives` (the six gradient-fit objectives and `HmmMetrics`, 1,019) and `estimation` (Baum-Welch, Viterbi and the compiled EM routes, 1,060), with `__init__` at 97. Each `__init__` re-exports every public name under an explicit `__all__`, so every `from sal.sample.potts_mcmc import X` and `from sal.opt.hmm import X` is unchanged; `potts_mcmc.__all__` now also lists `AdjacencyLists`, `adjacency_lists` and `bond_roots`, which were public and unlisted. Six private names are public in `potts_mcmc.sweeps`: `sweep_at` and `balanced_sweep_at`, which `chains` calls, and `GUARD`, `bond_probability`, `single_site_sweep` and `site_update`, which the tests pin. A test that replaces a kernel's collaborator now patches `sweeps` or `chains`, whose globals the kernel reads (#1010, C3).
