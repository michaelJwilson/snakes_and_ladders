`EmissionHmmObjective.value_and_gradient` takes its E step from `oxisal.ragged_posterior_probabilities` (issue #1253), the streamed core of `src/hmm_stream.rs` with each position's posterior kept: scaled probabilities in place of `ragged_posteriors`' log-space `log_add`s. Fat LTO, min of 3: 13.2 ms against 85.1 ms at 200 segments of 100-3,000 (317,033 positions), 0.60 ms against 3.25 ms at 200x60; a four-state negative-binomial value and gradient 47.9 ms against 119.6 ms and 4.17 ms against 9.19 ms. Against an 80-bit recursion on segments of 1,200-3,000 the marginals are within 4.6e-15 where the log-space kernel's are within 3.7e-11.
