`sal.search.decoding`: the maximum-posterior-marginal labelling
and the loss it minimizes (issue #696). Which estimator is wanted is decided by
the reported metric: `label_accuracy` scores per-site agreement, so the
marginal decoder is the matching one, while a maximum-a-posteriori labelling
minimizes the chance of getting the whole field wrong. On the 3x3 triangular
antiferromagnet, against enumeration of all 19,683 labellings, the two differ
at six of nine sites and each wins on its own loss --- the marginal labelling
expects 5.49 wrong sites against 6.01, while ranking 19,555th of 19,683 by
posterior. On a ferromagnet at the same field they agree exactly.
