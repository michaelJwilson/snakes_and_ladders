`PottsProblem.alpha_expansion` and `PottsProblem.trws`: the cut network and the TRW-S chain layout held in Rust after the first call, one crossing per solve (alpha-expansion crossed 9-21 times), the labellings, cycles and bound traces of `alpha_expansion` and `trws` bitwise; `then_icm=True` composes index ICM in the same call. `PottsProblem.from_directed_csr` holds a directed adjacency, so `SweepOrder.WORKLIST`, `FloorAt.EPOCH_GUARDED`, `MergeGain.HALVED` and `MergePairs.ADJACENT` reproduce the downstream labelling step's descent, floor and merge; `sal.qa.potts_labelling` runs its ICM, expansion, TRW-S, merge and anneal arms on one held problem.
