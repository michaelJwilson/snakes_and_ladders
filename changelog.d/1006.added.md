`sample.metropolis.random_walk`: gradient-free random-walk Metropolis over any objective, with `hmc.Adaptation` as its warm-up, `store_chain=False` and Kalman-filtered `operators`. On a declared energy (`sample.declared`: Gaussian, Rosenbrock) the chain, its warm-up and `Power` operators' filters run in Rust, at 0.03–0.37× BlackJAX's `rmh` runtime.
