The torch-route `sample.hmc.sample`, `anneal`, unadapted `parallel_tempering` and `langevin.mala` carry `grad U` at the current point from the previous transition, so a proposal evaluates `n_steps` gradients rather than `n_steps + 1` (MALA: 1 rather than 2), plus one at each start; draws are bitwise unchanged (issue #1222). `spent` counts the gradients evaluated, on the compiled routes too, which already carried them: `Integrator.force_evaluations` takes `carried=`, and `search.hmm_starts.CHARGES` read the new count. On a 200-segment Gaussian HMM a 20-proposal chain takes 501 ms against 646 ms for HMC and 135 ms against 241 ms for MALA.
