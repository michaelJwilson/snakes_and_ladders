Swendsen-Wang and Wolff cluster updates on the Potts lattice (`sal.search.potts_mcmc`), beside single-site heat bath behind one move-set interface, with the external field handled by a Metropolis accept step on the cluster's field difference rather than ignored. Each is validated by a chi-square goodness-of-fit against the exact Boltzmann distribution at an enumerable size, with and without a field, and the test's power is itself pinned by an ablation that removes the accept step. `sal.search.statistics` adds the chi-square tail probability and an integrated autocorrelation time, both written rather than taken from a new dependency and both pinned against published or closed-form values.
