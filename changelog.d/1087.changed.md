Notebooks re-execute at release, not on every pull request (#1087). The `notebooks` job runs `infra/check_notebooks.py --changed`: a changed notebook selects itself, a change under `docs/nb/data/` or to the checker selects all, and any other change runs none and installs nothing. `infra/release.sh` now re-executes every notebook; the full run was 590 to 1,438 s of a local CI run.
