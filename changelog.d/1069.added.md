`sandbox.potts_mip` (conserved, not adopted) solves a Potts ground state as a MIP over the local polytope by HiGHS (`scipy.optimize.milp`), whole or with sites outside a free region held at given labels, and `free_region` grows the LP's fractional sites by lattice rings (#1069). It is the enumerated minimum on 10 small lattices, frustrated ones included; its tests are `release`, the tiling-rung one skipped. On `spatio_tiling/release` it finds -17,022.1988 from 95 to 703 free sites; the full MIP's lower bound at 30 minutes, -17,022.82, puts the optimum in a 0.62-wide interval. `validation.scripts.highs.polytope` is public, the constraint matrix both share.
