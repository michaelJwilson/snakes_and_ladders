`validation.highs.mip` solves a Potts ground state as a MIP over the local polytope by HiGHS (`scipy.optimize.milp`), whole or with sites outside a free region held at given labels, and `validation.highs.free_region` grows the LP's fractional sites by lattice rings (#1069). It is the enumerated minimum on 10 small lattices, frustrated ones included. On `spatio_tiling/release` it finds -17,022.1988 from 95 to 703 free sites; the full MIP's lower bound at 30 minutes, -17,022.82, puts the optimum in a 0.62-wide interval.
