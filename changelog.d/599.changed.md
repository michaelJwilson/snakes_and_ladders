The Rust single-site Potts sweep is the default, on an exact pin (#599).
`search.potts_mcmc.sample_potts` gains the `backend` argument it lacked, and
it, `anneal_potts`, `parallel_tempering` and `adapt_ladder_potts` default to
`Backend.RUST`. The kernel now decides a site only where the draw clears
every cumulative boundary by 16 units of the last place per state, returns
the position of the first site it declines, and NumPy decides that one --
`search/kernels.py`'s construction, ported into `src/potts.rs`. The chain is
therefore the oracle's **state for state**, not a chain of the same law: 48
runs over two extents, four seeds and two alphabets agree on every recorded
configuration and the kernel hands back 0 of 2,457,600 sites, so no
committed chain, figure or fixture moves. `sample_potts` is 111x faster at
16x16 and 140x at 32x32, tempering 36x and 45x, annealing 14x and 17x.
`potts_mcmc_rust.sample_potts` keeps its name and is now one line of dispatch.
