Rust routes by default (issue #997), each pinned to the route it replaces:
- `baum_welch_family` streams Gaussian and count-family HMMs, with or without an integer covariate (`with_table`, `table_size`, `approx`, `stirling_from`).
- `viterbi` and `hmm_log_likelihood` are new.
- `mixture_log_likelihood` streams when no gradient is taken.
- `langevin.mala` compiles the chain for a declared Gaussian (`store_chain`).
- `simulated_bifurcation` runs in Rust.

Other changes:
- `GaussianHmmObjective.gradient` uses Fisher's identity.
- The emission-mixture EM runs on distinct counts.
- The alpha-beta swap cuts only the moving sites.
- `SetSurrogate` pools before its last affine map.

New goals were added against hmmlearn, scikit-learn, BlackJAX, rustworkx, TorchRL, PyG, JAX and gco.
