A ladder is a schedule: `sample.schedule.LadderTempSchedule` is a sequence of temperatures as a `TempSchedule`, `ladder()` reads either spelling into the tuple a run consumes, and the six temperings that took a bare sequence --- `sample.tempered`'s three ensembles, `sample.potts_mcmc.parallel_tempering`, `sample.hmc.parallel_tempering` and `sample.annealed.simulated_tempering` (a schedule read as temperatures and inverted) --- take a `TempSchedule` too. The same floats either way, so every chain, swap and estimate is bitwise what it was. `annealed_importance_sampling` and `population_annealing` keep the sequence alone: their ladder is anchored at `beta = 0`, the infinite temperature a `TempSchedule` refuses by design.
