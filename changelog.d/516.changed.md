The tests that referee `snakes_and_ladders.sandbox` sit beside it, in `tests/regression/sandbox/`: `test_sandbox_tropical.py` from the top level and `test_pruning_burn.py` from `tests/regression/likelihood/`. `infra/select_tests.py` gains `sandbox` as a module, so its dependents are derived from imports like every other module's — the sandbox imports `likelihood`, `search` and `sim`, so a change to any of those still runs the conserved gradient tape, and a change to the sandbox itself now runs its own tests, `qa`'s and its benchmark rather than the whole suite.

`tests/regression/test_sandbox.py` stays at the top level and joins `ALWAYS`. It asserts an absence — that none of `sim`, `likelihood`, `opt`, `search` or `learn` imports the oracle home — and an absence is not an import the selection can follow; it cost 1.9 s on a contended host, an upper bound.
