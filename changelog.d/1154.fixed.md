The ghost-spin and label-directed Swendsen-Wang sweeps decide a forbidden
label (a `-inf` field entry from `sal.sim.potts.forbid`). A forbidden label is
a hard constraint: a free ghost-spin cluster draws uniformly from the labels
every member allows, and the ghost couplings are shifted by the minimum over
allowed labels, so the chain no longer freezes from an allowed start. A
label-directed proposal onto a label any member forbids is rejected, one off a
forbidden label accepted. Neither computes `-inf - (-inf)` or `0 * -inf`;
every all-finite chain is bitwise unchanged.
