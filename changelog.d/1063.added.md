`validation.highs` solves the explicit local-polytope LP with HiGHS (SciPy's `linprog`) in a subprocess and checks `search.trws` against it: equal to 1e-8 relative wherever TRW-S converges to it, 13 instances up to 2,500 sites. At 5,041 sites and ten states the LP is -10,454.16 on `spatio_only/release`, integral, in 344 s, and -17,022.93 on `spatio_tiling/release`, fractional, in 268 s, 2.1 GB peak each: TRW-S stops 0.05 below the latter, so 0.75 of its 0.81 bracket is between the LP and the best labelling. Both runtimes are `Goal`s for `trws`, which takes 0.82 s and 8.7 s (issue #1063).
