`sim.potts.SiteField` declares a field's sign: `SiteField.from_log_weight(h)` for the log-weight every sampler and solver reads, `SiteField.from_energy(H)` for an energy, negated once, and `SiteField.widened` for a field shared by every site. `sample_potts`, `anneal_potts`, `wolff_sweep`, `swendsen_wang_sweep`, `niedermayer_sweep`, `alpha_expansion` and `iterated_conditional_modes` take one, or a bare array read as a log-weight as before. The coupled model's own Wolff move is folded into `wolff_sweep` on `SiteField.from_energy`, bitwise the same labels.
