Every iterative result carries a `Termination`, never `None` (#1085, T1). **Breaking:** the `termination` field of the 16 result types that had one (`FitResult`, `MultiStartResult`, `EmFit`, `CategoricalFit`, `MixtureFit`, `EmissionMixtureFit`, `Labelling`, `ExpansionResult`, `MethodRun`, `Certificate`, `SpatioSequentialFit`, and the `projection`, `bifurcation`, `max_cut` and two `infer` results) is required and keyword-only. A loop of a fixed step count (the annealers, tempering, the cluster-move replicas) reports its budget; a single cut move reports one completed iteration; `run_descent` reports ICM's own. `map_tasks` defaults to the serial loop, `workers=1, pool="serial", intra_op_threads=None`, so `map_tasks(f, items, generator=rng)` runs as written (T2).
