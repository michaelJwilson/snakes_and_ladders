The message-schedule seam is guarded rather than remembered: a class shaped like a schedule that does not inherit `likelihood.schedule.MessageSchedule`, a schedule the registry does not reach under a `MessageScheduleName`, or a consumer branching on a schedule's name now fails `tests/regression/test_duplication_guards.py`.
