Compiled chains run the package's own value+gradient kernels, at any temperature (issue #1220). An objective opts in through `supported_gradient() -> (kernel, data) | None`, which replaces the `DeclaredGaussian`, `DeclaredRosenbrock`, `DeclaredMixture` and `DeclaredGaussianHmm` protocols and their `gaussian_precision`, `rosenbrock_constants`, `gaussian_mixture_declaration` and `gaussian_hmm_declaration` attributes; `sample.declared.declared_energy` stays the one reader and returns the kernel name and its data. `oxisal.SupportedEnergy(kernel, data, dimension)` evaluates one, and the Gaussian mixture, Gaussian HMM and count mixture objectives' gradients call it, so a chain and a fit share one arithmetic; the count mixture (negative binomial, beta-binomial, count pair) now compiles. `hmc.sample`, `langevin.mala` and `metropolis.random_walk` take the compiled route at any positive temperature; at `T = 1` every compiled chain is bitwise unchanged. `oxisal.HmcWalk`, `MetropolisWalk` and `leapfrog_trajectory` take `(kernel, data)` where they took `(family, parameters)`, and the walks take `temperature` after `powers`.
