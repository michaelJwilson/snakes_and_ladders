`sample.metropolis.random_walk` takes a `np.random.Generator` where it took a `torch.Generator`, and its Python route evaluates the objective on arrays through the new `opt.objective.energy_of` (#1011, step 3). `energy_of` calls an objective's declared `energy(x: np.ndarray) -> float` (`opt.objective.DeclaredEnergy`) and falls back to `float(objective(torch.as_tensor(x)))`, so every objective still runs; `Rosenbrock` and `validation.gaussian.GaussianTarget` declare one, within 6.1e-16 relative of `__call__` (bitwise at `d <= 3` off the dense Gaussian). `random_walk` landed in #1006 after release 0.3.0, so no deprecation shim is kept. A seed now gives a different chain on both routes; `replay` is bitwise unchanged against BlackJAX's `rmh`.
