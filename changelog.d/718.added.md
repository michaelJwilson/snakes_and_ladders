The spectrum of a coupling graph as one seam, and a spectral lower bound on the two-state ground-state energy with the start it gives (`snakes_and_ladders.sim.spectrum`, `snakes_and_ladders.search.spectral`, issue #718). The bound holds for every coupling sign --- one eigenvalue of the coupling matrix, the field lifted onto a ghost spin, tightened by the Delorme--Poljak diagonal correction --- so the frustrated lattice and the planted glass, where minimum cut does not apply, gain a second lower bound beside the dual one: 0.75 of the closed-form optimum on the frustrated lattice, -14.51 against the enumerated -14 on the glass. Dense `eigh` is the oracle the sparse `eigsh` route is pinned to at 1e-10.
