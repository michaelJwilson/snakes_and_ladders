`qa.hmc_warmup`: the convergence of an adapted Hamiltonian Monte Carlo chain
against a fixed-step one on a correlated Gaussian whose mean and covariance
are declared, so both are refereed by a closed form. Both are correct
samplers; the warm-up buys 2.08e-02 effective draws per gradient against
5.85e-03, its own discarded gradients charged to it.
