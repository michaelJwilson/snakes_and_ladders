The Rust heat-bath sweep takes a field of one row per site, so `Backend.RUST`
runs the per-site field `spatio_only` declares; it took one shared row and
refused the rest. `beta` is applied to the accumulated local field, where the
Python oracle applies it, rather than pre-scaled into the field and the
couplings: the two orderings differ in the last bits on 45.3% of sampled
entries, so the backends agreed bitwise only at `beta = 1.0` before. At 5,041
sites and 10 states the sweep runs 0.63 ms against the Python sweep's 33.57 ms.
