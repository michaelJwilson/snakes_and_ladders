`src/count_mstep.rs`: the count families' M-step solves in Rust, each `sum_u w_u (digamma(u + x) - digamma(x))` evaluated as `sum_j T_j / (x + j)` over the tails of the weights, with no special function. `emissions.M_STEP_BACKEND` defaults to it; `Backend.PYTHON` runs the batched torch solves, kept as its oracle.
