`search.bifurcation.simulated_bifurcation`: a Potts ground state by simulated bifurcation, one oscillator per site and label integrated at once, on NumPy or on `torch` (`Backend.TORCH`, new) with the labelling pinned equal across the two. A row of `search.ground_state`'s table, refereed by the cut at two labels, enumeration at nine sites and on the declared glass, and the dual bound where neither reaches; the force scale is read from the instance, since the dense-matrix scale returns the field-only labelling on a sparse graph in a field.
