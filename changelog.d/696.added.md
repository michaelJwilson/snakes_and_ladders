`sal.search.tightening`: a lower bound on a Potts ground-state
energy, and the plaquette or triangle clusters that tighten it (issue #696).
The bound is valid at every iteration by construction rather than at
convergence. On the 3x3 square lattice it closes to zero and **certifies the
labelling optimal** at both signs of coupling, including the non-submodular
one minimum cut cannot take. On the frustrated triangular lattice the pairwise
relaxation returns the same bound whatever the coupling --- with three states
every triangle is 3-colourable, so it satisfies every edge at no cost --- and
adding the triangles closes 80% and 77% of the remaining gap at `J = -0.8` and
`J = -1.5`. Unlike a region-based free energy this is a bound and not an
approximation, which is what lets it certify.
