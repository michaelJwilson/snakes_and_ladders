`snakes_and_ladders.search.gibbs`'s heat-bath sweep runs through a `numba` kernel
over an edge layout by default, 122x the NumPy sweep and 7.1x a
`sample_factor_graph` run at 32x32. `gibbs_sweep`, `sample_factor_graph` and
`anneal_factor_graph` take `backend`, whose `Backend.PYTHON` is the oracle the
kernel reproduces bitwise: every chain is the chain it was.
