`sal.sample.loop` holds one annealing loop, `anneal`, and one tempering loop, `temper`, over a `Step` that carries its energy and an opaque per-state value (a gradient, or nothing) and charges each move in its own unit (issue #1218). `hmc.anneal`, `potts_mcmc.anneal_potts`, `gibbs.anneal_factor_graph`, `gibbs.anneal_topology`, `hmc.parallel_tempering`, `potts_mcmc.parallel_tempering` and the three `tempered` ensembles are constructors of a step on these loops, each bitwise what it was; `swap_log_ratio` moves to the loop and stays exported from `potts_mcmc`.
`metropolis.random_walk` charges its start energy (`per_start=1`): `spent` is one more per chain, three more with an adaptation on the NumPy route. `anneal_factor_graph` and `anneal_topology` record the shared loop's `track` series (best state, temperature, best energy).
