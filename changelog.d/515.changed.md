The per-site-field Potts problem is named `spatio_only`, not `potts_spots`, so the name states the intent --- the spatial half of the coupled model --- rather than the mechanism, and does not read as a variant of the abstract Potts lattice (issue #515). The fixture directory is `tests/regression/fixtures/spatio_only/`, the declared model is `spatio-only`, and `snakes_and_ladders.sim.potts` exports `SpatioOnlyParams`, `spatio_only_field` and `load_spatio_only_params`.
