`sal.emissions.rising.scaled_rising_table(shapes, counts)` returns `S(x, m) = lgamma(x + m) - lgamma(x) - m log x` at every shape and count, `(len(shapes), len(counts))`, with `gammaln(x)`, `log x` and the series' term count formed once per shape; it is `scaled_rising_array(shapes[:, None], counts[None, :])` bit for bit. `bb.trial_tables`, `nb.count_log_factor` and `nb.exposure_table` build through it, their tables unchanged bit for bit: at K = 8 and extent 2000 the beta-binomial tables take 0.49 ms at tau = 10 (1.20 before) and 0.87 ms at 1e12 (1.68 before), min of 3.
