`search.maxflow_rust` runs Boykov--Kolmogorov, and a batch entry point takes the pool (issue #715). Four kernels were built in Rust behind one seam --- Dinic, highest-label push-relabel, Boykov--Kolmogorov and a synchronous parallel push-relabel on rayon --- pinned to the Python Dinic arc for arc and timed on the cut and as the inner solver of alpha expansion. Boykov--Kolmogorov won both tables: 61 ms on the 256x256 cut against Dinic's 234 (fitted exponent 1.03 against 1.15 in the site count), 352 ms against 637 on the 64x64 x 10-label expansion. The other three are conserved in `sandbox.maxflow_declined` behind the `sandbox` Cargo feature. `ising_ground_states` solves a batch of fields on rayon with the GIL released, 2.16 s to 0.90 s for eight 256x256 cuts from one thread to four; inside one cut no thread pays, and the parallel kernel says so with its number. `alpha_expansion` is unchanged in interface and 1.8x faster on the 64x64 x 10 instance.
