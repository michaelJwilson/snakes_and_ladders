Two study scripts measure tuned HMC annealing (`step_size="auto"`, a tuned
ramp, the polish from its best) against restarts with the same polish at equal
passes: `sal.qa.mixture_hmc_anneal` on the five-component mixture, and
`sal.qa.count_pair_hmm_anneal` on a seven-state count-pair HMM. Findings in
`docs/experiments/030`.
