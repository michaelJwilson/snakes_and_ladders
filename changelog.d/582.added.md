`tests/regression/fixtures/tree_scale/` declares the leaf-count ladder #582
asked for: 20 leaves on a pull request, 50 behind `-m stress`, 200 in the
release process. It is a **cost** fixture, not a difficulty one --- neighbour
joining recovers the declared topology exactly at every rung, RF 0.0000 --- and
what it measures is the size this branch's post-order optimizations are paid
for at: 8.389 ms to 7.032 ms per gradient at 20 leaves, at an identical
log-likelihood of -23642.15298199 on identical evaluation and fit counts.

A fixture's `tau` may now be declared as `{balanced: n, height: h}` rather than
written out node by node, naming `sim.tree.balanced_tree`'s two arguments. That
function is deterministic, so the declaration fixes the tree exactly as a
literal one does without carrying six hundred lines for the 200-leaf rung. The
height bounds every root-to-tip path, so the rungs differ in leaf count and in
nothing else: holding the edge length fixed instead grows the diameter with the
leaf count until distant pairs saturate.
