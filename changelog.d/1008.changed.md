`hmc.sample` and `langevin.mala` run the whole chain compiled on any declared energy — Gaussian, Rosenbrock, one-channel Gaussian mixture, Gaussian HMM — with `Adaptation` and Kalman-filtered `Power` operators, and on an HMM objective's JAX energy under `jit`. The torch route resolves its gradient route once per type, cutting a Rosenbrock warm-up chain from 1.70 s to 1.04 s. `oxisal.gaussian_hmc` and `gaussian_leapfrog` are replaced by `HmcWalk` and `leapfrog_trajectory`. A chain on a mixture or HMM objective now draws from the compiled stream, not torch's: the same posterior, a different seeded chain (the `hmc` seeding in `docs/nb/spatio_sequential.ipynb` moves from 2.2 to 3.8 nats short).
