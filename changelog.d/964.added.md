`sample.relabel` relabels the draws of a mixture posterior by the six methods of `label.switching` — STEPHENS (the default of `relabel`), ECR, ECR-ITERATIVE-1, ECR-ITERATIVE-2, PRA and SJW — and the ordering constraint, for any emission family through `allocation_draws`. `sample.mixture_gibbs` adds a conjugate Gibbs sampler for the Gaussian mixture, and `opt.emission_mixture.EmissionMixtureObjective` a differentiable mixture likelihood over positive family parameters, so HMC and MALA sample a count mixture. `docs/nb/label_switching.ipynb` reads every method against the generating components.
