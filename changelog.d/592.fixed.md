`sum_product(graph, schedule="tree")` ran *flooding*. `schedule` was typed as a
`MessageSchedule` and compared with `is`, and a plain string compares equal to
a `StrEnum` member without being it, so a named schedule silently fell through
to the other branch and a caller got a different algorithm than the one asked
for. `schedule.resolve` makes the name, the member and the instance one call,
and a test pins all three to the same answer.
