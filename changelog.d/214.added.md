`snakes_and_ladders.sim.graph.erdos_renyi_graph` draws `G(n, p)` beside `lattice_graph`, and belief propagation is now measured over an ensemble rather than on three hand-built fixtures. Acyclicity is checked per draw so the oracle stays exhaustive enumeration and no asymptotic threshold is invoked: BP is exact on every acyclic draw to 3.7e-15 relative, and the deviation on cyclic draws is reported. The ensemble reaches structures no committed fixture did — 104 of 120 draws carried an isolated vertex, the case on the boundary of the edgeless special path.
