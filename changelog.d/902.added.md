`sample.initialize.FromTempering` takes `calibration: LadderCalibration | None`, a warm-up that settles its Hamiltonian ladder by `schedule.adapt_ladder` (exchange acceptance into a band, rungs added up to `max_replicas`) or `schedule.adapt_ladder_by_round_trips` (up-fraction, fixed length), each measurement a short `hmc.parallel_tempering` run from the start's own generator; what it settled on and spent, in transitions, gradients and seconds, is kept on the start as `spent` (`TemperingSpend`, `CalibratedLadder`). `n_rounds` may be a `Budget` in `Cost.SECONDS`, the rounds running until the longest so far would pass it, through a `deadline` on `hmc.parallel_tempering`. The default stays `None`, and the existing tempering pins hold unchanged. On the two-Gaussian weight posterior the fixed ladder `(1, 64)` exchanges at 0.067 and the calibrated `(1, 8, 64)` at 0.373 and 0.327 inside the band `(0.2, 0.6)`, the cold replica 0.13 and 0.41 standard errors from quadrature; on `emission_mixture/ci` the calibrated start's handover is below the fixed ladder's at equal transitions on 4 of 8 seeds, a mean of -1.25 in log-likelihood.
