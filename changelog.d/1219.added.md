`hmc.sample`, `hmc.anneal`, `hmc.parallel_tempering` and the `FromChain`, `FromAnnealing` and `FromTempering` starts take `step_size="auto"` with a `StepTuning` (issue #1219): `sample.tune.tune_step` runs one pilot per step of a log grid on the shared `Step` loop, ranks them by the ESJD per gradient or the lowest energy reached, and charges the pilot in `spent`; tempering tunes each rung. On the HMM starts the tuned steps reach the truth's basin in 3, 3 and 5 of 10 seeds against the grid's 10, 10, 10, so `search.hmm_starts.STEP` and `ANNEALING_STEP` stay.
