The MCMC chains count their cost one way: `Chain`, `HmcChain`, `LangevinChain` and `SliceChain` carry `spent` in a declared `unit` (was `force_evaluations`, or `objective_evaluations` on `SliceChain`): gradients for HMC and Langevin, objective evaluations for the random walk and slice sampling. `HmcChain` and `LangevinChain` are subclasses of `Chain`, and `run_chain` and `run_compiled` take the kernel's `unit` (#1090).
