A Potts chain reports whether it mixed (#1316, Part A). `PottsChain` carries
`acceptance`, `largest_cluster_share`, `ess` per observable and a
`termination`, ending `Stop.NOT_MIXING` with its draws kept when an effective
sample size falls under 50. `sample_potts_starts` runs the ordered, drawn and
equilibrated starts and reads split R-hat (`sample.statistics.split_rhat`)
against 1.01. `sample_potts` takes an optional `start`. On #1314's 64 x 64
instance the ordered Niedermayer chain accepts 1 of 320 moves and R-hat is 30.4
to 95.9.
