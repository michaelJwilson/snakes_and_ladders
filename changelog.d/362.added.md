Bicycle codes as a second construction of the parity-check problem class: `sim.ldpc.bicycle_code` builds `H = [A | A^T]` from a sparse circulant and deletes rows to the target rate, as edges rather than as an array, so the 19,998-bit code draws without a 200 MB matrix. The channels, the decoder and the oracles are the Gallager code's, and three fixtures at 12, 96 and 996 bits match the Gallager fixtures in length, degrees and design rate. The construction is refereed exactly -- degrees, `k = n - rank H` across four deletion counts, the self-orthogonality `H H^T = 0` over GF(2), and minimum distance 4 over the 12-bit fixture's 64 enumerated codewords -- and the decoding is compared with a Gallager draw on shared seeds: more blocks failed at all six 96-bit settings and at eleven of twelve 996-bit ones.
