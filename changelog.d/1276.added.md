`sal.sim.potts` gains four exact square-lattice results as referees
for a Potts sampler at lattice scale: Onsager's and Kaufman's `q = 2` energy
per site (the plane and the `L x L` torus), Yang's spontaneous
magnetization, and Baxter's critical energy for `q = 2, 3, 4`. Swendsen-Wang,
Wolff, their heat-bath variants and the single-site control are checked
against them at `64 x 64` per pull request and at `128 x 128` and
`256 x 256` for a release.
