`EmissionMixtureObjective` builds through the family (issue #1164): it maps `theta` by each parameter's declared `Domain` and rebuilds by `with_parameters`, so a Gaussian, binomial or categorical mixture is now an objective as a count mixture was. **Deprecated:** its `build` argument and `opt.emission_mixture.build_like` warn with a `DeprecationWarning` and are removed one release later; omit `build`. A count family's `theta`, value, gradient and compiled route are unchanged bit for bit (70 of 70 arrays over six count families, with and without a covariate).
