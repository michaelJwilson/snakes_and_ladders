The count families' M step forms its profiled mean as a matrix-vector product
rather than an elementwise sum over an `(n_obs, n_states)` intermediate (#649).
At the coupled model's declared scale that intermediate is 80.7 MB per call,
and the rewrite is **13.72x faster** there: 54.9 ms to 4.0 ms. A matmul reduces
in a different order, so the fit moves a relative 2.7e-16 from the conserved
family's — five orders inside the 1e-11 this repository declares for a float64
comparison. `CLAUDE.md` gains the rule that permits it: bitwise is what a
comparison strives for, the declared tolerance is the floor it may back off to,
and a difference outside that tolerance is still a defect.
