The HMC and random-walk warm-up no longer raises when a coordinate does not move in its recorded draws (issue #1207). The mass diagonal is the inverse of Stan's regularized variance, `(n / (n + 5)) var + 1e-3 (5 / (n + 5))` over `n` recorded draws, on the torch, JAX and Rust paths; coordinates whose sample variance was exactly zero are reported on `Adapted.flat` and the compiled walks' `flat`. Seeded adapted chains move.
