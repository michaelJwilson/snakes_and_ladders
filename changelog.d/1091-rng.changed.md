Every torch sampler and learner takes the package's `rng` where it took a `generator`: the HMC `sample`, `anneal` and `parallel_tempering`, `calibrate_ladder`, `mala`, `slice_sample`, `goemans_williamson`, `gumbel_softmax`, `estimate_gradient`, `optimize`, `fit_surrogate` and `curriculum`. A `torch.Generator` passed as `rng` runs the chain it ran before; a NumPy generator is turned into a stream by `torch_stream`. `infer` and `parsimony_search` require `rng`, which was optional (#1091).
