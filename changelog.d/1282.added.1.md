`external.session(solver, *, transport=...)` (#1282, steps 2 and 2b): one persistent worker per session, `python -m sal.external.worker`, imports its framework once and serves every call over pipes, so a call no longer pays interpreter and import start-up. `Transport.MMAP` (the default) passes each array through a memory-mapped file the parent creates and unlinks; `Transport.NPZ` keeps the `.npz` protocol. Inputs are C-contiguous `float64`, `int64` or `bool`, refused otherwise before the call; results are bitwise equal across transports and the one-shot `invoke`.
