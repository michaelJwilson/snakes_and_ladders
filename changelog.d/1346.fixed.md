Two count-fit degeneracies end Baum-Welch with `Stop.DEGENERATE` instead of raising (#1346). A negative-binomial dispersion solved at or under `emissions.mstep.DISPERSION_FLOOR` (`float64` epsilon) is reported in `Reestimate.degenerate` and ends the fit with the last valid parameters, the state named in `EmFit.unsettled.states`; the input whose bracket underflowed to `r = 0` raised `ParameterDomainError` (Rust) or `math domain error` (torch). A beta-binomial state under two mean trials holds its concentration and re-estimates its rate, where it held both before, and the fit ends `DEGENERATE` naming it.
