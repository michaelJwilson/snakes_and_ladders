The textbook's appendix gains **The Analytic Results the Implementation Rests On** (issue #809): eleven results the code treats as truth, each with the assumption an instance can violate and the statement that uses it --- the Jukes--Cantor transition matrix and the saturation distance its inverse is undefined above; the Hadamard conjugation, which shares no traversal with the pruning recursion and is therefore an independent answer rather than a second spelling; the transfer matrix, exact in the model's structure and exponential in a strip's width; maximum-flow/minimum-cut duality, which is the optimum at two labels and a bound beyond them; the BCJR extrinsic split, which is what stops the turbo iteration feeding a decoder its own belief; channel polarization, whose frozen set is earned at one channel parameter and not at another; the exponential family's Bregman divergence, which must reduce to k-means++ on a Gaussian and nowhere else by accident; the Gumbel-softmax, exact at the corners and nowhere between; McNemar's exact test on discordant pairs; the chi-square test, whose independence assumption is why a thinning interval is part of the test; and the enumeration counts, which decide what "enumerable" means and therefore where a claim changes its referee. Five results already derived in the Proofs appendix are listed rather than repeated.
