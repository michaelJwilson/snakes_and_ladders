Polar codes: the Kronecker transform, the frozen set chosen by Bhattacharyya recursion on the erasure channel or by the Gaussian approximation, and successive-cancellation and successive-cancellation-list decoding. The construction is refereed by invariants that do not run out with the code length — capacity is conserved exactly under the transform, and `polar(8, 4)` is the same object as `RM(1, 3)` and the extended Hamming `(8, 4)` code, three constructions meeting at one code — and the decoders against exhaustive maximum likelihood where it is affordable: SCL at list size 16 equals ML on 300 of 300 words, plain SC on 297, and SC is exactly SCL at list size 1.
