Polar codes, conserved as a declined route in `snakes_and_ladders.sandbox`
(`polar`, `polar_decoding`, `polar_reference`; issue #593): the Kronecker
transform, the frozen set chosen either by Arikan's exact erasure recursion or
by the Gaussian approximation, and successive-cancellation and
successive-cancellation-list decoding. Reed--Muller is the same transform under
the weight rule rather than a second construction, and at `N = 8`, `k = 4` the
two rules meet at one code, the extended Hamming `(8,4)`.

The construction is refereed by what does not run out with the length: capacity
is conserved **exactly** under the transform, `sum_i I(W_i) = N (1 - eps)` to
1e-12 at `n = 3, 5, 8, 11`. The decoders are refereed by enumeration at the
declared `N = 16` instance, where the gap successive cancellation leaves is
measured rather than cited --- 98 block errors against maximum likelihood's 62
over 200 shared draws at `sigma = 1.0`, of which a list of four recovers 34.
`SCL(1)` is `SC` bitwise and `SCL(2^k)` is maximum likelihood, which pins the
fork-and-prune machinery at both ends; `polar_reference` is the naive recursion
the list layout is pinned to, bitwise over 900 draws on three channels.

`sec:polar` states the model, the sizes, the factor graph and the validation.
Nothing outside `tests/` calls the route, its instance is the file beside its
tests rather than a row of the fixture registry, and `PROBLEMS.md` names no row
for it. CRC-aided list decoding is the ticket's fourth step, not taken.
