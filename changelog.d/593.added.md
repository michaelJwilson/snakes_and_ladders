Polar codes (`snakes_and_ladders.sim.polar`,
`snakes_and_ladders.likelihood.polar`, issue #593): the Kronecker transform,
the frozen set chosen either by Arikan's exact erasure recursion or by the
Gaussian approximation, and successive-cancellation and
successive-cancellation-list decoding. Reed--Muller is the same transform
under the weight rule rather than a second construction, and at `N = 8`,
`k = 4` the two rules meet at one code, the extended Hamming `(8,4)`.

The construction is refereed by what does not run out with the length:
capacity is conserved **exactly** under the transform,
`sum_i I(W_i) = N (1 - eps)` to 1e-12 at `n = 3, 5, 8, 11`. The decoders are
refereed by enumeration at the declared `N = 16` instance, where the gap
successive cancellation leaves is measured rather than cited --- 98 block
errors against maximum likelihood's 62 over 200 shared draws at
`sigma = 1.0`, of which a list of four recovers 34. `SCL(1)` is `SC` bitwise
and `SCL(2^k)` is maximum likelihood, which pins the fork-and-prune machinery
at both ends; `likelihood.polar_reference` is the naive recursion the list
layout is pinned to, bitwise over 900 draws on three channels.

`sec:polar` states the model, the sizes, the factor graph and the validation;
`polar` joins `PROBLEMS.md` as the repository's eleventh problem class.
CRC-aided list decoding is the ticket's fourth step and needs the elementary
codes' CRC.
