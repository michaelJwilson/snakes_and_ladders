The sizing harness Milestone 2.1's gate needs first
(`sal.opt.failure`, issue #596). The gate asks for a learned
policy that beats the classical baseline, and on every fixture in the tree the
baseline is **1.000**: random-restart hill climbing reaches the enumerated
maximum from every start at the budget a comparison holds equal. Nothing beats
an exact answer, so the first work is sizing --- growing an instance until the
baseline measurably fails, and recording the size at which it first does.

`probe_failure` runs a method from seeded starts at one budget and reports how
many reached the target; `failure_curve` sweeps sizes and names the first that
falls below a threshold. Three refusals carry the discipline: a method that
spends more than its budget is refused rather than rounded, a probe stopped by
its wall-clock ceiling reports the starts it ran rather than the starts it was
asked for, and a baseline that never fails reports `first_failure is None` ---
the finding issue #596 says to bring the curve for, not a missing result.

Refereed by the measurement that motivated the ticket: on the 7-taxon tree at
60 decisions a single greedy descent reaches the enumerated maximum from 0.560
of 50 starts and random restarts from **50 of 50**, reproducing #194's 1.000.
It sits beside `opt.budget` rather than in `qa/`, where the plan first put it,
because a script in `qa/` renders what the application computed and a sizing
sweep computes.
