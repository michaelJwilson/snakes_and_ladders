One API per problem and one gateway per algorithm: the #1059 audit's steps (issue #1059).

The continuous samplers share one result shape (step 1). **Breaking:** `HmcChain.theta`, `LangevinChain.theta` and `SliceChain.theta` are renamed `draws`, the name `Chain` and `metropolis.random_walk` already used. `sample.langevin.mala` takes `operators` and `LangevinChain` carries `expectations`, filtered as `hmc.sample`'s are. `sample.slice.slice_sample` takes `temperature`, `store_chain` and `backend` (`PYTHON` alone), and refuses an `adaptation` by name; at `temperature=1.0` every chain is bitwise the one before. **Breaking:** `metropolis.random_walk` takes `rng` where it took `generator`, and `theta0` as a `np.ndarray`; the module imports no torch at run time, as it takes no derivative. The returned `Chain` still holds tensors, since its loop is the one HMC and MALA share.

Samplers leave `opt/` and evaluators join `likelihood/` (step 2). **Breaking:** `draw_assignments`, `anneal_assignments` and `AnnealedAssignments` move from `opt.emission_mixture` to `sample.mixture_anneal`; `viterbi` and `hmm_log_likelihood` move from `opt.hmm` (`opt.hmm.estimation`) to `likelihood.hmm`. `opt.hmm.compiled_family`, the compiled kernels' family name and parameters, is public so both directories read one table. Every result is bitwise unchanged; the code moved without an edit.

Backend twins live at `<sub>.<backend>.<algorithm>` (step 2b). **Breaking**, no shims: `opt.hmm_jax` is `opt.jax.hmm`; `likelihood.pruning_jax`, `pruning_torch` and `pruning_rust` are `likelihood.jax.pruning`, `likelihood.torch.pruning` and `likelihood.rust.pruning`; `likelihood.{message_passing,ragged,convolutional,spatio_sequential}_rust` are `likelihood.rust.{message_passing,ragged,convolutional,spatio_sequential}`; `sample.hmc_jax` is `sample.jax.hmc`; `sample.kernels` splits into `sample.numba.gibbs` (`gibbs_sweep_sites`) and `sample.numba.factor_graph` (`factor_graph_log_density`); `search.maxflow_rust` is `search.rust.maxflow`; `sim.count_pairs_rust` is `sim.rust.count_pairs`. `backend.twin` resolves `<sub>.rust.<algorithm>`. Outside a twin's own subpackage only its gateway and `tests/` import it, which `test_duplication_guards.py` reads from the package's, `infra/`'s and the notebooks' imports; the torch pruning module's five importers in `learn`, `qa`, `search` and `sandbox` are admitted by name until its gateway is decided. Every default backend is unchanged, so every result is bitwise the one before.

`backend` means `sal.backend.Backend` alone (step 3). **Breaking:** `parallel.map_tasks` and `search.infer.infer` take the pool as `pool=` where they took `backend=`; `parallel.BACKENDS` is `parallel.POOLS`, and the `parallel.Backend` alias of `Pool` is gone. `likelihood.pruning.log_likelihood` gains a `Backend.TORCH` door, the taped module's value at the lengths `tau` carries, bitwise the direct call and within the float64 agreement bound of the NumPy oracle; `qa.backend_agreement` iterates `Backend.PYTHON`, `TORCH` and `RUST` through it rather than dispatching on strings, and its figure's numbers and labels are unchanged.

Learners share one entry shape (step 4). **Breaking:** `learn.reinforce.reinforce` takes `iterations`, `batch` and `max_steps` by keyword; `learn.tabular.q_learning` and `sarsa` take `rng` where they took `generator`; `learn.rollout.rollout(environment, policy, rng, *, max_steps, start, stop_at_local_optimum)`, `greedy_rollout(environment, *, max_steps, start)` and `greedy_restarts(environment, rng, *, max_steps, start)` put the generator after the environment and the budget and start behind `*`. Every result is bitwise the one before; `phylo_tree.ipynb` and `potts_chain.ipynb` re-execute to the same outputs.

One class or helper per concept (step 5). **Breaking:** `likelihood.belief_propagation.ConvergenceError` is gone; `likelihood.message_passing.ConvergenceError(method, iterations, residual, tolerance)` is the one refusal belief propagation, flooding, its reference and the sandboxed Kikuchi iteration raise, and it carries the cap, the residual and the tolerance on each. Its message now reads `<method> did not converge in <n> iterations: largest message change … against a tolerance of …` on every route. `sample.chain.torch_stream(rng)` is the torch generator seeded from a NumPy one that `search.projection` and `search.mixture_starts` each defined. **Breaking:** `sample.gibbs.Annealed` is `AnnealedLabelling` and `sample.hmc.Annealed` is `AnnealedTheta`.

Every compiled kernel releases the GIL (step 6). `oxisal.ragged_posteriors` and `oxisal.leapfrog_trajectory` run inside `py.detach`, as every other `#[pyfunction]` did, so `pool="threads"` runs them concurrently; results are bitwise unchanged. `tests/regression/test_gil_release.py` runs each on four threads and reads the calling thread's longest pause: on the kernels as they were it was 0.41 s against a 0.10 s call and 1.89 s against a 0.41 s call, over the 0.5-call bound; released, both pass. The `numba` kernels already carried `nogil=True` since #1055.

Tree search names its start `start` (step 7). **Breaking:** `search.infer.infer` and `parsimony_search` take `start=` where they took `topology=`, the name `sample.gibbs.anneal_topology` and `sample.tempered` already give it.

`opt.hmm.CategoricalFit` carries `termination` (step 8), the `Termination` `EmFit`, `MixtureFit` and `EmissionMixtureFit` already carry, on both routes of `baum_welch`. Additive: it defaults to `None` and the four-tuple an unpacking reads is unchanged.

One EM budget (step 8b). **Breaking:** `opt.em.EmConfig(max_iterations=500, tolerance=1e-12)`, frozen, with `opt.em.EM` its default instance, replaces the separate `max_iterations=` and `tolerance=` of `opt.mixture.expectation_maximization`, `opt.hmm.baum_welch`, `baum_welch_family`, `opt.emission_mixture.expectation_maximization`, `opt.split_merge.split_and_merge`, `sandbox.rectangular_hmm.baum_welch_rectangular`, `sandbox.annealed_em.annealed_expectation_maximization` and `opt.em.em_loop`; each takes `config=`, and a caller changes one field with `dataclasses.replace(EM, tolerance=...)`. The three count-mixture sites keep their 200 / 1e-10 as `opt.em.EMISSION_MIXTURE_EM`, so every result is bitwise the one before; whether they move to `EM` is open (#1059, Q4b). `opt.fit.fit` and `fit_from` keep `max_iterations` and `gradient_tolerance`: they run L-BFGS, not EM.

Potts samplers and solvers share `field`, `start` and `cluster_backend` (step 9). **Breaking:** `sample.potts_mcmc.anneal_potts` takes `start=` where it took `initial=`; `search.alpha_expansion.expand`, `alpha_expansion`, `swap`, `alpha_beta_swap` and `search.bifurcation.simulated_bifurcation` name their field `field` where they named it `field_values`. `sim.potts.check_labelling` is the one start check: `search.ground_state`, `anneal_potts`, `alpha_expansion`, `alpha_beta_swap`, `expand`, `swap` and `search.icm.iterated_conditional_modes` call it, so each refuses a start that is not one integer state in range per node with one sentence; `anneal_potts`, expansion, swap and ICM accepted a float start before and now refuse it. `annealed_importance_sampling`, `population_annealing`, `simulated_tempering`, `tempered_potts_pair` and `sample_potts_pair` take `cluster_backend`, handed to `sweep_for`, with the default `PYTHON` each ran before, so every result is bitwise the one before; whether `RUST` becomes the default is open (#1059, Q5′).

Potts ground-state termination (step 10). `search.alpha_expansion.Labelling` carries `sweeps` and `termination`; `search.icm.iterated_conditional_modes` fills both on either backend, the termination converged where a sweep would leave the labelling unchanged. `search.ground_state.descend` is one ICM call reading `sweeps`, where it reran ICM one sweep at a time: bitwise without a floor; under a `min_sites` floor it follows ICM's own rule, so a sweep whose move the floor undoes is not clean, and the generator is left where one call leaves it. **Changed:** `alpha_expansion` and `alpha_beta_swap` at `max_cycles` warn and return the labelling they hold, `cycles = max_cycles` and a `Termination` recording the budget, where they raised `ValueError`; `ground_state`'s budget-derived caps now return rather than raise.
