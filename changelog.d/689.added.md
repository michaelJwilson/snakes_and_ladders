`snakes_and_ladders.likelihood.region_graph`: regions over a factor graph,
their counting numbers by Mobius inversion, and the Kikuchi free energy over
them (issue #689). The construction reproduces the pairwise case it
generalizes, which is what referees it: at the Bethe region graph `-F_K` is
`log Z` to 8.9e-16 on a chain and the Bethe value `message_passing` reports to
1.8e-10 on a 3x3 and a 4x4 lattice, and the singleton counting numbers come
out `1 - d` without being told. The plaquette region graph the Kikuchi
approximation is worth taking on a square lattice is built and checked; the
parent-to-child updates that look for a stationary point of this energy are
the next step of the ticket. Kikuchi is not a bound and the module says so.
