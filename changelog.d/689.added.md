Generalized belief propagation over a region graph, with the Kikuchi free
energy as its objective, conserved as a declined route in
`snakes_and_ladders.sandbox.region_graph` (issue #689): it does not settle at
size, so nothing outside `tests/` calls it. On the 3x3 Potts lattice at three
states, against enumeration of all 19,683 configurations, the plaquette regions
put `log Z` within 6.66e-06 where the pairwise ones reach 1.11e-02 --- 1,672x
nearer at `J = 0.6`, for 3.7x the wall clock --- and the advantage decays with
coupling, 23,378x at `J = 0.3` to 130x at `J = 1.2`. The construction is
refereed by the case it generalizes: at the Bethe region graph the free energy
is `log Z` to 8.9e-16 on a tree and the updates find `message_passing`'s fixed
point to 1.65e-10. Kikuchi is not a bound and the module says so.
`numerics.logsumexp` now reduces several axes in one call, which is what
marginalizing a region belief onto a child needs.

At size the constraint is convergence rather than accuracy: on the 6x4 strip
the plaquette messages settle at `J = 0.25`, where they are 14,219x nearer the
exact `log Z` for 28x the wall clock, and do **not** settle at `J = 0.5` or
`0.875` at any damping from 0.7 to 0.98 over 20,000 sweeps. `app:kikuchi` and
`app:gbp` state the free energy and its stationary point; a `pytest-benchmark`
case carries both region families at both sizes.
