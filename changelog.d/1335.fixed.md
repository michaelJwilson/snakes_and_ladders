The negative binomial's count tables hold the scaled rising factorial `S(r, y)` and the exposure term is written as `y log(μc / (1 + μc/r)) − r log1p(μc/r)`, one order on every NumPy and Rust route, so no term of size `r log r` cancels at large dispersion; the beta-binomial M step's scores use `digamma_rising` (#1335).
