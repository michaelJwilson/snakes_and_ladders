`m_step` re-derived the covariate's slice rather than calling
`likelihood.spatio_sequential.covariate_block`, and appended the trailing
singleton unconditionally (#670). On an `(S, V, 2)` covariate that gives
`(n_m, S, 2, 1)`, whose last axis names no channel, so the pair family refused
it: the params admitted a covariate, the E step scored with it, and the M step
could not re-estimate under it. `covariate_block`'s own docstring names this
mistake; `m_step` was the one seam of three that did not call it.

Refereed by recovery. Over an exposure spanning a factor of 16, a coupled fit
**told** the exposure recovers planted rates of 24.0 and 72.0 as **23.3 and
71.3**, within 3%; the same fit on the same data **not told** it converges to
**47.9 and 151.7** -- factors of 1.99 and 2.11 against an `E[U(0.25, 4)]` of
2.125, the rate averaged over the exposures. The stale slice is kept as a test
of its own refusal, so the expression cannot come back.
