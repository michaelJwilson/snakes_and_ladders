`search.spatio_sequential.fit_spatio_sequential` defaults to `backend=Backend.RUST`, and the Rust route of `likelihood.spatio_sequential.{class_posteriors, external_field, labelled_log_likelihood}` serves Gaussian, negative-binomial and beta-binomial classes, which it refused. The kernel reads one table per call, keyed by count, by `(count, trial count)` pair, or by site (`rust.one_channel_table`), each the family's own `log_density`; a fit builds it once per M step. At `spatio_sequential/stress.yaml`, min of 3, the Rust fit is 2.21x (negative binomial under exposure) to 2.94x (negative binomial) the NumPy fit over seven families and covariates; the other three defaults stay `PYTHON`, at 1.09x, 1.68x and 1.14x on the beta-binomial under trial counts. `log_prior` is vectorised over edges (543 us to 13.1 us at 180 edges, bitwise) and the Rust route's Potts term is `oxisal.coupled_log_prior` (1.21 us, bitwise). The categorical and two-channel count paths are bitwise unchanged; the Rust route agrees with the NumPy oracle to 1.9e-14.
