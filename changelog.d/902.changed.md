A sixth test kind, `experiment`: a comparison of two runs of the package, methods, starts or settings on a declared instance at a stated cost, pinned where it was measured. Its referee is neither a truth nor the implementation's own contract, so it is not `smoke`, and like every kind but `end2end` and `oracle` it counts toward no coverage. Registered through `infra/gates.py` and `infra/test_kinds.py`; the tempering start's calibration against the fixed ladder on `emission_mixture/ci` is the first carrier.
