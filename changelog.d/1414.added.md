`sal.qa.combined_solvers`: a two-panel solver figure and table. Panel (a) is the Potts labelling: argmax, index ICM, the downstream path (worklist, guarded floor, halved adjacent merge), ICM with floor, alpha-expansion+ICM, TRW-S's decode, the anneal under heat bath, Wolff+Gibbs and Swendsen-Wang+Gibbs at `T0 = J d / 3`, and the merge alone, each before and after ICM and the merge. Panel (b) is the count-pair HMM: starts (the run's own, restarts, k-means++, quantile, HMC anneal and sampler) polished by `CountPairHmm`/`PhasedCountPairHmm` under the downstream fit, then to convergence. Every solver runs through `PottsProblem` and the count-pair HMM classes. `sal.external.stream` captures a downstream package's stream realizations through its own stage functions, as a runtime-only import run in a subprocess (`sal.validation.scripts.stream`), with no extra and no lock entry. Without the package, the figure runs on the `potts_labelling` and `count_hmm_reference` fixtures. `docs/experiments/041-*.md` reports 25 realizations.
