`search.maxflow.max_flow` and `search.maxflow.ising_ground_state` default to `Backend.RUST` (was `Backend.PYTHON`). Labellings, energies and cuts are unchanged, bitwise; the flow value differs by summation order, 8.2e-13 relative at 512². On an open 512² lattice `ising_ground_state` takes 92 ms against 29.0 s, `max_flow` 217 ms against 28.4 s. The `RUST` route leaves the network's capacities unchanged; a caller that reads the residual capacities passes `backend=Backend.PYTHON`.
