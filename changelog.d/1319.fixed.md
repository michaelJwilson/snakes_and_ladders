`sal.sample.statistics.integrated_autocorrelation_time` returns `1 + 2 sum rho(t)`, as its docstring stated, where it returned Sokal's `1/2 + sum rho(t)`: independent draws give 1, not 0.5. Every effective sample size read as `n / tau` (`PottsChain.ess`, `TemperedSupport.autocorrelation_time`) halves, and every standard error read as `std sqrt(tau / n)` grows by `sqrt(2)`; the truncation lag of Sokal's window is unchanged. Five Wolff referees on 64 x 64 now fall under the effective-sample floor of 50 (#1320).
