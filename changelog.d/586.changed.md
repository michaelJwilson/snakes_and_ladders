`PottsGraph.compressed_adjacency` returns the same read-only arrays on every
call instead of deriving them per call, so a caller that wrote into them now
raises rather than editing the graph; a caller that needs to write takes a
copy. A Wolff cluster move on a 64x64 periodic lattice is 3.69x faster.

`FactorGraph.degree` and `.neighbours` read a stored incidence instead of
scanning every factor: 15.1x and 19.1x over 800 variables, and linear rather
than quadratic in the graph. `degree` now counts incidences, so a variable a
factor names twice counts twice, and an unknown name raises `KeyError` rather
than reporting zero.

`likelihood.message_passing`'s plan keeps its lists of lists: building the
whole layout is 0.6 per cent of a `sum_product` over a 2,000-variable chain,
0.24 on a 2,000-leaf star and 0.22 on a loopy 16x16 lattice. The module
carries the measurement so it is not re-opened, and the cost the profile does
rank -- the tree schedule, 24.4 per cent at 2,000 variables -- is #592.

`numerics.logsumexp` reduces a one-dimensional vector of scores with
`axis=0`. Fourteen sites across seven modules wrote `values[None, :]` and
indexed `[0]` back off to reach the same number; the values, the arithmetic
and the results are unchanged.

Every `Protocol` the package declares is now named as a base by its implementers — `class LinearPolicy(TrainablePolicy)` — so `Protocol.__subclasses__()` is the implementer list the deleted `SEAMS.md` used to print, and it is right where the derived one was wrong: `Schedule` has 5 implementers rather than 0 (its members are `__call__` and a property, which a structural walk cannot see), and `Policy` has 4 rather than 11 (a one-method protocol matched every emission family that happens to define `sample`). Each seam marks its methods `@abstractmethod`, so an implementer that forgets one fails at instantiation instead of at a later call site; value-like `@property` members stay unmarked, since an implementer may supply a value as a dataclass field and a seam that dictates the representation has stopped being a seam. The protocols remain `typing.Protocol`, so structural typing, `isinstance` and duck-typed implementations outside the package all keep working. `TrainablePolicy` re-declared `sample`, the single member `Policy` has, and is now stated as the subclass it always was.
