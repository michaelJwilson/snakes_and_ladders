`likelihood.objective.BranchLengthObjective` takes `gradient=`, choosing
between the taped derivative of `likelihood.pruning_torch` and the closed form
of `likelihood.pruning_analytic`. The two agree at 5.4e-16 relative through the
objective and fit to the same optimum; the default stays the tape, because
experiment 012 found the ratio between them inverting on the branch lengths
alone, 2.11x to 0.57x at one tree and one site count, so no default follows
from a problem size.
