The textbook's derivations appendix derives each of the three exact evaluators the main text cites rather than states: Felsenstein's pruning as the marginalization over internal states (`app:pruning`), forward–backward as the same marginalization on a chain (`app:forward-backward`), and sum-product on a tree with the Bethe free energy of a factor graph as its loopy stationary point (`app:sum-product`, `eq:bethe-factor`); the pruning, forward–backward, path-enumeration and message-passing modules cite them.
