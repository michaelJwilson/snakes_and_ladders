A rate–concentration reading of the beta-binomial (#1205): `BetaBinomialEmission.rate_concentration()` and `CountPairEmission.rate_concentration()` return `RateConcentrationBetaBinomialEmission` and `RateConcentrationCountPairEmission`, which score as the `alpha, beta` family at `alpha = concentration * rate`, `beta = concentration * (1 - rate)` and name `rate` (`Domain.PROBABILITY`) and `concentration` (`Domain.POSITIVE`). `Restricted(objective, at, coordinates(objective, ["rate"]))` then holds the concentration fixed in `EmissionHmmObjective` and `EmissionMixtureObjective`.
