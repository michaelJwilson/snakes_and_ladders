`likelihood.ragged.posteriors` takes `switch=`, one probability per position: the step into position `t` takes `(1 - s[t]) I + s[t] A`, the stay-or-switch form, built per step inside the Rust kernel from the one `(K, K)` transition rather than a `(T, K, K)` stack. It agrees with the oracle's materialized stack to 1.8e-15, and a constant switch is the plain call with the mixed kernel, bitwise at `s` in {0, 0.35}. On 64 segments of 500 at four states it takes 31.2 ms, against 526.1 ms for the per-step stack through the oracle.
