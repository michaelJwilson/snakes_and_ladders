`Reestimate.emissions` is renamed `components`, the name #1163 and #1176 gave the fitted family in the HMM objectives, Baum-Welch, the decoders and the covariate updates (issue #1197). The field keeps its position, so positional unpacking is unchanged; a caller reading `step.emissions` or constructing `Reestimate(emissions=...)` reads `step.components` and writes `Reestimate(components=...)`. `HmmParams.emissions` and `SpatioSequentialParams.emissions` are a different concept and keep their name.
