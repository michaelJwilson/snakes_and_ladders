`sal.opt.hmm.CountPairHmm`: the unphased count-pair HMM as one compiled object (`src/count_pair_hmm.rs`) holding data, emission, chain and buffers, so `fit` runs Baum-Welch in one FFI crossing and returns an `EmFit`; with `posteriors`, `m_step`, `log_likelihood` and `viterbi`. Selectable: one dispersion and concentration across states (`tied`, solved in Rust), the initial distribution and transition held, a parameter-change stopping rule, and a joint L-BFGS emission M step (`MStepSolver.LBFGS`) beside the per-block one.
