`snakes_and_ladders.numerics_rust.sample_rows` borrows its arrays instead of copying them, through `rust-numpy`. The Python-visible speedup over the NumPy oracle rises from 1.3x to 2.6x at 2,000,000 draws and from 2.2x to 2.8x at 200,000, and no longer shrinks as the array grows. The result is unchanged: it remains bit-identical to the oracle for the same generator state.
