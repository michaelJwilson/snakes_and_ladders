`EmissionFamily`'s three data-facing members --- `sample`, `log_density` and
`reestimate` --- take an optional `covariate`, one conditioning value per
observation, conditioned on and never fitted (#631). All seven implementers
accept `None`, which scores exactly what passing nothing scores; a supplied
covariate raises `CovariateNotSupportedError` rather than being dropped, since
a covariate silently ignored fits a different likelihood than the caller asked
for. No family conditions on one yet: the exposure in `NegativeBinomialEmission`
and the trial count in `BetaBinomialEmission` are the steps that follow.
