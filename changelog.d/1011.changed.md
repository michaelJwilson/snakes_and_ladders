`sample.expectation` is NumPy in and NumPy out, and imports no torch (#1011): `KalmanMean.update` and `update_block` take array-likes, and `Expectation.mean`, `standard_error` and `phi` are `np.ndarray` where they were `torch.Tensor`. `Expectation` landed in #988 after release 0.3.0, so no release carried the tensor fields and no deprecation shim is kept. `sample.declared` drops torch too: `Power` applies to an array or a tensor, and `DeclaredGaussian.gaussian_precision` is any array-like. On 418 recorded estimates the means, `phi` and `n` of every row-by-row stream and compiled declared operator are bitwise unchanged; 8 of 67 standard errors move by 1 ulp, where torch's vectorized `sqrt` is not correctly rounded (80 of 12,800 inputs on AVX512, NumPy's 0). `update_block` now adds a block row by row, so it is bitwise the one-at-a-time filter (192 of 192, and the compiled route's undeclared operators against the stored draws) where torch's cascade sum held it to 2.1e-13 relative.
