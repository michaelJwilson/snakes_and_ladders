A `Runtime optimization audit` issue template (`.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/optimization.yml`): a repository-wide ticket that profiles every module's workloads at stated size tiers, checks `CLAUDE.md`'s optimization opportunities (cache, layout, SIMD, branches, inlining, allocation, double buffering, the FFI boundary, `numba` or Rust) against each ranked loop, and then implements what the measurements license, each change pinned to its oracle with both numbers reported.
