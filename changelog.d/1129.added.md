`sal.param_tree`: nested parameters as one tree, with the package's own API — `flatten`, `unflatten`, `structure`, `map_leaves`, `stack` over restarts or seeds, and `named` for dotted leaf paths, plus `register` to make a frozen dataclass a node. `optree` (Apache-2.0, a new dependency approved on #1129) is the backend, reached only there, and dicts are walked in insertion order. On an `HmmParams`-sized tree it round-trips in 12.4 us and stacks 64 restarts in 121 us, against 151 us for the stack written out by hand; dm-tree took 15 us and 327 us and reads a frozen dataclass as a leaf. The first consumers are the count pair's `named_parameters`, `search.infer`'s NumPy view of a fit, and the surrogate trainer's best-state copy, each bitwise the loop it replaces.
