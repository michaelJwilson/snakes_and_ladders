The unit a method spends is one vocabulary: `Cost` moves from `infra/ladder.py` to `sal.cost`, `opt.budget.Budget.unit` is a `Cost` rather than a `str` spelled eight ways, and the five units a budget named that no rung spends --- `decisions`, `fits`, `site visits`, `passes`, `candidates` --- are members. `Comparison.table()` prints what it printed for six of the eight; `"site-visits"` and `"site visits"` were one unit under two spellings and are now `site visits`, and `"evaluations"` is the ladder's `objective evaluations` (#860).

Whether a loop finished and why is one `opt.termination.Termination` --- `converged`, `iterations`, a `Stop` of three values --- carried as an additive trailing field on fourteen optimizer results that answered it five ways between them. Every existing field keeps its value; `None` is *not known*, and the rows that cannot say which branch ended their loop leave it.

`likelihood.pruning.log_likelihood` takes a `backend`, defaulting to `Backend.PYTHON`: `Backend.RUST` is `pruning_rust`'s own call, bitwise on `tree_jc/ci` and `tree_search/ci`, weighted and unweighted. `forward_backward` takes none --- the ragged kernel returns the transition counts summed over segments where the evaluator returns them per step, so the two do not share a return --- and `ragged_rust.posteriors` takes one instead, selecting between the kernel and the oracle it is pinned against. No rung merged and no arithmetic moved.

One refusal and one name for a pool: `backend.refuse_backend(name, backend, allowed)` carries the sentence seven sites wrote inline, each message byte-identical; the three sites whose refusal names no alternative --- `sample.gibbs`, `sample.potts_mcmc` and `search.alpha_expansion` --- keep their own words. `backend.py`'s docstring states which modules default to RUST, NUMBA and PYTHON. `parallel.Backend` is `parallel.Pool`, the old name kept as an alias, so the word `Backend` names which implementation runs a kernel and nothing else (#860).
