A Gaussian mixture on data with zero spread is built and fitted instead of
raising. `pooled_variance_floor` returns `ZERO_SPREAD_FLOOR * max(1, |mean|)**2`
where the observations are all equal, and a channel whose observations are all
equal is named as `flat` on `GaussianMixtureObjective`, on `GaussianEmission`
and on `MixtureFit`, following the warm-up's `Adapted.flat` (#1207): its scale
sits at the floor and it does not count as a collapse.
