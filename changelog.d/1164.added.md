Every emission family declares its parameter domains and rebuilds itself (issue #1164): `EmissionFamily.parameter_domains()` maps each name of `named_parameters()` to a `Domain` (`REAL`, `POSITIVE`, `PROBABILITY`, `LOG_SIMPLEX`), and `with_parameters(named)` returns the family at those parameters with its constants (trial counts, tie, joint form, variance floor) kept. `sal.opt.constrain.constrained` and `free_from` apply the map a `Domain` names.
