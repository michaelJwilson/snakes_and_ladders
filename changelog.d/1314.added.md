An enumeration oracle holds the Niedermayer move to the exact Boltzmann law
under a per-site field N(0, 1) at 1.5 of the critical coupling (2x3, q = 3,
pooled chi-square p = 0.133; with the accept step dropped, p = 0.0). The
move's gap of 206 standard errors from Glauber on 64 x 64 is a frozen chain,
not a defect: from the ordered start its cluster holds 4,070 to 4,084 of
4,096 sites and 0.0% to 0.3% of moves are accepted, over three seeds.
`sample/CLAUDE.md` records the limit.
