A ladder is validated once: `sample.schedule.check_ladder(values, needed_by=..., minimum=..., monotone=..., quantity=..., from_zero=...)` replaces the five validators that checked four requirements in four combinations --- `schedule`'s and `tempered`'s `_check_ladder`, the inline copies in both parallel temperings and `annealed`'s `_check_betas` --- with every requirement an argument. Two refusals are reworded where two of the five spelled the same requirement two ways: a non-positive rung is refused in the words `_check_temperature` has always used (#861).
