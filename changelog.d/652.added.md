A per-observation covariate reaches the emission families from the caller
(#652). #631 gave the families an exposure and a trial count and refereed them
at the family; nothing above them passed one, so no fit could condition on one
and no end-to-end claim rested on them. Three seams now thread it.

`SpatioSequentialParams.covariate` is `(S, n_nodes)`, the observations' own
axes, defaulting to `None` — the model every construction before this field
described. `gated_log_density` passes it to every class's family,
`class_log_density` slices it by the same member block as the observations, and
the spatial M step selects the same members and moves the same axis, so a
family always scores and re-estimates each member against that member's own
exposure.

`baum_welch_family` takes a `(n_sequences, length)` covariate and reaches both
seams of the loop, the E step's scoring and the emission M step: a fit that
scores against an exposure and re-estimates without it is fitting two different
models. The HMM objectives store one beside their observations, for the same
reason the observations are there — it is fixed for the life of the objective,
where the family is rebuilt from `theta` on every call. A family that
conditions on nothing, `CategoricalEmission` and `PoissonEmission` among them,
refuses a covariate rather than ignoring it.

Refereed end to end, which is the point of the ticket: over a `U(0.25, 4)`
exposure whose draw spans 15.8x, a negative-binomial Baum-Welch told the
exposure recovers a planted 2.0 and 9.0 as 1.9688 and 8.8088, and the same fit
on the same data not told it converges to 4.1505 and 18.5904 — the rate
averaged over the exposures, factors of 2.075 and 2.066 against an
`E[U(0.25, 4)]` of 2.125. A constant exposure of ones scores bitwise, and
through 200 EM iterations moves the fit a relative 1.5e-13 at the single thread
`tests/conftest.py` pins, two orders inside the 1e-11 declared for a float64
comparison. That figure is stated with its thread count because it does not
survive one: the same fit reads 5.1e-14 at two threads and 9.9e-14 at four,
since a split reduction sums in a different order again.

`test_the_spatial_params_carry_the_covariate_to_both_seams` claimed in its name
and docstring that both spatial seams "change when the params' covariate does",
while its body asserted finiteness and a constructor error. The canonical
instance is categorical and refuses a covariate, so there was no fixture to make
that claim against — which under No Coverage Theatre makes it a gap to ticket
rather than a sentence to write over an `isfinite`. Renamed to what it checks,
and the `critical` marker dropped with the claim: a test that gates early has to
be one whose failure means the rest is not worth running, and this one's failure
means an import broke. #658 carries the gap and the four seams that a
covariate-carrying spatial fixture would have caught.
