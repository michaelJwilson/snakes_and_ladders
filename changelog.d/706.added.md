`snakes_and_ladders.learn.potts_nd.PottsNDEnvironment`: an N-dimensional Potts
lattice at known parameters with a **per-site** external field, whose action is a
move and the temperature it runs at (issue #706). The single-site arm lands here;
the Wolff and Swendsen-Wang arms and the mixed move set follow.

Two properties make it comparable with the classical suite rather than beside it.
Its score is the negation of `sim.potts.energies` **bitwise**, so the learner and
`search.ground_state` score one problem. And the baseline is a point in the action
space: repeating the sweep action at temperature zero reproduces
`iterated_conditional_modes`'s labelling label for label, which is what makes a
*match* against ICM provable rather than arguable.

`snakes_and_ladders.learn.keyed` carries the mechanism that allows it: a move's
randomness is keyed on a digest of the state and the action, never streamed, so
`Environment.step` stays the deterministic function the protocol declares and
`learn.exact`'s enumeration requires -- the obstacle #597 left open for the
k-armed bandit, settled without widening the protocol.
