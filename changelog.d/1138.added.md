`sal.likelihood.ragged.viterbi` (#1138): the most probable path of every segment of a `Ragged` log-density, with the arguments of `posteriors` and every `SwitchKind`. Returns `Paths` (`path`, `log_joint`); ties go to the lower state, as `hmm.viterbi` breaks them. The compiled route is `oxisal.ragged_viterbi`; `viterbi_oracle` is the NumPy oracle.
