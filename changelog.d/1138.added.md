`sal.likelihood.ragged.viterbi` (#1138): the most probable path of every segment of a `Ragged` log-density, with the arguments of `posteriors` and every `SwitchKind`. Returns `Paths` (`path`, `log_joint`); ties go to the lower state, as `hmm.viterbi` breaks them. The compiled route, `oxisal.ragged_viterbi`, decodes segments in parallel with the GIL released and takes a Kronecker step in its factors; `viterbi_oracle` is the NumPy oracle. It matches enumeration over every path (lengths 2 to 6), `hmm.viterbi` bitwise on a rectangular batch, and the oracle path for path. Over 2e5 positions at ten states it takes 20.4 ms against the oracle's 1.52 s (74x), 65.2 ms against 1.88 s with a stay-or-move switch (29x), and 12.7 ms against 5.54 s under `KRONECKER` (434x).
