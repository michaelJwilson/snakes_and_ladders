`GaussianEmission` holds a collapsed state instead of raising, as the negative binomial and beta-binomial do since #1136 (issue #1160). A state whose re-estimated variance reaches `variance_floor`, or whose posterior mass falls below `COLLAPSED_MASS`, keeps its last mean and scale and is listed in `Reestimate.frozen`, and from there in `EmFit.frozen`, `EmissionMixtureFit.frozen`, the new `MixtureFit.frozen`, `MixturePolished.frozen` and `Seeding.frozen`. This reverses the default #122 set: a fit that met the floor raised `ValueError` and now returns, naming the state. `GaussianEmission(..., on_collapse=Collapse.CLAMP)` sets the variance to the floor and re-estimates the mean; `on_collapse=Collapse.REFUSE` keeps the #122 raise. `oxisal.gaussian_em_step` returns each state's posterior mass as a fifth array, and the compiled Baum-Welch routes hold an emptied state's transition row rather than returning a NaN fit.
