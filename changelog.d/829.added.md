`sim.simulator`: one way to simulate a problem, `Simulator = (params, rng) -> dataset`, with `SIMULATORS` keyed by the declared model as `sim.fixtures.LOADERS` is --- eight models --- and `NOT_SIMULATED` naming the seven with no simulator and why. Every entry is the call its problem always drew with, so a fixture's draw is the same bits; `sim.hmm.simulate_sequences` takes a generator beside its params and builds one from the seed when handed none. `Simulator` is exported from the package root beside the other seams.
