`EmissionHmmObjective` replaces the six per-family HMM objectives (issue #1189). `HmmObjective`, `GaussianHmmObjective` and the Poisson, binomial, beta-binomial and negative binomial objectives are deprecated: each warns with `DeprecationWarning`, builds the `EmissionHmmObjective` at the start its `initial()` took (`opt.hmm.family_start`) with its old `Backend.JAX` default, and is removed after the next release. `EmissionHmmObjective` gains `backend` (`RUST` by default, `JAX`, `TORCH`), `theta_from_truth`, the streamed Gaussian `gradient`, `gaussian_hmm_declaration` and `jax_energy`; the JAX route (`opt.hmm.jax`) reads the family from its start.
