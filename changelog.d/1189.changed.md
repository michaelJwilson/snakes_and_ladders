`EmissionHmmObjective` replaces the six per-family HMM objectives (issue #1189). `HmmObjective`, `GaussianHmmObjective` and the Poisson, binomial, beta-binomial and negative binomial objectives are deprecated: each warns with `DeprecationWarning`, builds an `EmissionHmmObjective` at the start its `initial()` took (`opt.hmm.family_start`), and is removed after the next release. The JAX route (`opt.hmm.jax`) and the compiled Gaussian HMM declaration (`gaussian_hmm_declaration`) read the family from `EmissionHmmObjective`'s start.
