`sal.likelihood.schedule`: the order messages go in is an
interface now, not two branches. Five schedules, each declaring a `Guarantee`
of `EXACT`, `PARTIAL` or `APPROXIMATE` rather than a boolean — `tree`,
`upward` (the leaf-to-root pass alone: Felsenstein pruning, exact `log Z` for
1.71x less work), `downward` (the root-to-leaf pass alone, an ablation),
`flooding` and `sequential` (Gauss-Seidel over the same update). A sixth is
added by registering it; nothing in `message_passing` changes.

A schedule that does not compute a marginal leaves it **absent** from
`Marginals.variable` rather than present and wrong, so reading one raises; one
that cannot reach `log Z` reports `nan`. `sim.factor_graph`'s six adapters
already land every graph class on one representation, so the seam reaches the
Potts lattice, the hidden Markov chain, the phylogenetic tree, the coupled
model, the Tanner graph and the trellis without a seventh adapter.
