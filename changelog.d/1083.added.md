`emissions.CountPairEmission`'s independent form takes a covariate per channel, shape `(..., 2)`: channel 0 the total's exposure and channel 1 the successes' trial count per observation, in `sample`, `log_density` and `reestimate`. It scores each pair as scipy's negative binomial at mean `mu * c` plus beta-binomial at its own trials, to 1e-12 relative, and agrees bitwise with `sim.count_pairs.IndependentCountPair`. The split, `split_covariate` with `ChannelCovariates`, `TOTAL` and `SUCCESSES`, moves to `emissions.base` and is re-exported from `sim.count_pairs`. The joint form still refuses a covariate; a call without one is unchanged.
