`search.ground_state.lattice_rung` and `uniform_ground_energy`: the Potts lattice
as an instance family indexed by size, at the exact critical coupling, with the
closed-form optimum its zero-field member has (issue #596). `opt.failure`'s probe
gains a **relative** allowance, and its reading is now one-sided: a target may be
the best value *known* at a size rather than an optimum, and a start that beats it
has not failed.

The measurement renames the baseline for this problem. Against alpha-expansion's
energy, best of eight starts, single-descent ICM sits **5.31%** above it at 144
sites and **10.59%** at 576 --- and is unchanged at 25x the budget, so the failure
is structural rather than starved. Restarts close half of it. Wolff degrades with
size in a field, **3.38% to 34.35%**, being blind to the unary term. Simulated
annealing reaches the expansion's energy at every size and budget measured, so it
is the baseline a learned policy has to beat.

Two instances are excluded from that gate and the exclusion is the result: in zero
field the optimum is `-J|E|` in closed form, and at two labels in a field
alpha-expansion equals the exact graph cut bitwise to 1,024 sites. Nothing
outperforms a closed form.
