A tiling-field Potts fixture (#1050): `spatio_tiling/{ci,release}` splits a lattice into connected tiles, a seeded Voronoi tiling (`sim.potts.tile_partition`), and plants one favoured state per tile at a declared strength (`sim.potts.tiling_field`), a field not linear in the state, so the ten-state optimum is not a two-state cut. `search.potts_starts.tiling_rung` builds its rung; `recovery` and `recovery_bound` read a labelling against the planted states, the bound being the strength above which every alpha-expansion and alpha-beta-swap local minimum labels a tile its state in full. Enumeration referees the bound at `ci` (531,441 labellings); the planted states referee it at `release` (5,041 sites). `potts_starts.ipynb` gains a section, "A tiling field", running every solver on it.
