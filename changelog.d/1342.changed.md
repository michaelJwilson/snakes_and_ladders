The compiled rising-factorial kernels (`log_rising`, `scaled_rising_table`, `digamma_rising`) enter numba's on-disk cache (#1342): scipy's `gammaln` is bound by a registered symbol rather than a `ctypes` pointer, so a second process loads `log_rising` in 6.8 to 7.6 ms after numba's 0.21 to 0.28 s start, against 0.57 s compiling. Outputs are unchanged bit for bit.
