The torch-route `sample.hmc.sample`, `anneal`, `parallel_tempering` and `langevin.mala` evaluate the objective only for their forces: `U` at the current point is carried from the previous transition and at the proposal from the last force evaluation (issue #1217). On a 200-segment Gaussian HMM a proposal takes 64 ms against 74 ms for HMC and 21 ms against 37 ms for MALA; chains on the Gaussian, Rosenbrock, mixture and HMM targets are bitwise unchanged.
