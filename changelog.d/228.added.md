An emission-family interface for hidden Markov models (`sal.emissions`), with the categorical matrix as one implementation and univariate Gaussian emissions as the second. `HmmParams` now carries a family rather than a matrix; `GaussianHmmObjective` fits the continuous case, and `baum_welch_family` runs EM over any family. The Gaussian likelihood is unbounded, so its variance floor is derived from the data and reaching it raises rather than clamps.
