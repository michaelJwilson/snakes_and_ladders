One `opt.starts.Polished` (the value reached and the polish's `Termination`) and one `opt.starts.Trial` (seconds and state bytes) for every polish and timed fit. `opt.starts.PolishedPoint` adds the polished `theta`, `search.mixture_starts.MixturePolished` the components, weights and log-likelihood trace, `search.projection.ProjectedTrial` and `search.mixture_starts.MixtureTrial` what each fit records. The mixture's `converged` and `iterations` are read off its termination.
