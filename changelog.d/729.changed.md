Coverage counts a test only when it judged something. `infra/coverage_recut.py` recuts a run written with `--cov-context=test` to the tests carrying `oracle`, `end2end`, `mathematical`, `edge_case`, `infra`, `bug` or `backend`, with `qa` exempt and `search` held to its own floor; CI runs it on the push to `main` and `infra/release.sh` at the release gate. The marker scheme changes with it: `simulated_truth` is `end2end`, `structural` is retired in favour of `infra` and `smoke`, and `bug`, `warning`, `backend` and `baseline` are registered as the finding axis (issue #729).
