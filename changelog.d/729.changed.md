Coverage counts a test only when it judges the science. `infra/coverage_recut.py` recuts a run written with `--cov-context=test` to the tests carrying `end2end` or `oracle`, with `qa` exempt and `search` held to its own floor; CI runs it on the push to `main` and `infra/release.sh` at the release gate, and root `CLAUDE.md` states the rule. The marker scheme changes with it: `simulated_truth` is `end2end`, `mathematical` is `analytic`, `structural` and `edge_case` are retired in favour of `infra` and `smoke`, and `patch`, `backend`, `bug`, `warning` and `snapshot` are registered as the finding axis (issue #729).

The `search` package's tests are audited against the referee each one names, and the three modules no judging test entered gain oracles (issue #729, step 2). Judged coverage with `qa` exempt goes **83.08% to 83.75%** and `search`'s own **87.07% to 89.99%**; the complement --- every kind and finding but `end2end` and `oracle` --- goes 89.31% to **89.29%** over the whole and 88.66% to **88.96%** for `search`, the whole's 0.02 falling to what the judging set took. The floors in `infra/gates.py` are raised to 83.7, 89.9 and 88.9, the whole's unjudged floor standing at 89.2. `search/kernels.py` goes 12.68% to 97.18% against `likelihood.potts.log_weights`, the enumerated ground state and the enumerated Boltzmann marginals; `search/surrogate.py` 79.82% to 97.37% against `enumerate_potts` and a refitted maximized log-likelihood; `search/projection.py` 92.27% to 94.48% against the components a planted draw was made from. Fourteen tests judged against something outside the implementation and marked `smoke` are remarked `oracle` or `end2end`, and one rewrite that added no referee is reverted.
