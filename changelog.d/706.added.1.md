The Wolff and Swendsen-Wang control arms of
`sal.learn.potts_nd.PottsNDEnvironment`, reached through
`sal.sample.potts_keyed` (issue #706). A Wolff action names its
cluster root and the colour it recolours to; a Swendsen-Wang action names a
temperature and nothing else, which makes it six candidates a decision and the
smallest environment in which learning a schedule is the whole question.

These are `sample.potts_mcmc`'s own sweeps rather than second copies: above zero
temperature the move delegates to them and the labelling matches a direct call
**bitwise**. At zero temperature the bond probability is one, so a cluster is a
like-coloured connected component and the accept step keeps only a recolouring
that does not lower the score -- a limit ``beta = 1 / 0`` cannot express and the
one an oracle can check exactly.

Each arm walking `search.ground_state`'s declared exponential ladder is
statistically indistinguishable from `run_swendsen_wang` and `run_wolff` over 32
seeds, in the energy reached and in the site visits spent, which is what makes a
*learned* schedule measurable against a declared one.

A feature map is now the arm's: the columns are those the move set and the ladder
let vary, since a Swendsen-Wang arm's actions differ in their rung alone and a
gain column there would sit in the direction a softmax cancels.
