The Potts `parallel_tempering` and `cluster_tempering` take one ladder order, coldest first: a ladder in any other order is refused by `check_ladder`, naming the expected order, where `parallel_tempering` accepted any order before. Both take `start` as `(n_nodes,)` for every rung or `(n_rungs, n_nodes)` per rung, and under `temperatures="auto"` a per-rung start is mapped onto the tuned rungs by nearest temperature.
