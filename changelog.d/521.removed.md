The host lock and the scheme it served (`infra/locks.sh`, 135 lines, and its 234-line guard). Development runs one agent at a time on all four cores, so there is no contention to arbitrate. Measured, the lock did neither of the things it was built for: during an exclusive run 2.68 cores were busy and 1.75 of them foreign, `with_lock measure` gave up after its full 1,800 s wait while shared slots ran ahead of it, and taking `measure` for `infra/baselines.py --write` cost 30 minutes of queueing for a job that runs in 28 s. `tests/conftest.py`'s one BLAS thread per process is deliberately unchanged: every baseline in `STATUS.md` and `DEV.md` was taken at one thread, so widening it would stale all of them and is a separate decision.
