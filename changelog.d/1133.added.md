`sal.likelihood.ragged.posteriors(..., switch=s, switch_kind=SwitchKind.KRONECKER)` takes the phased two-layer transition `A ⊗ S_t`, and `SwitchKind.KRONECKER_DIAGONAL` the variant switching only on `A`'s diagonal (#1133). The kernel takes each step in its factors, never materializing a `2K x 2K` matrix. It agrees with the materialized-stack oracle and with enumeration over every path. Over 50 segments of 2,000 positions it takes 272 ms at K = 4 and 4.73 s at K = 16, against 460 ms and 7.63 s for an explicit `2K x 2K` step: 1.7x and 1.6x, bounded by the transition counts, which stay over all `2K x 2K` pairs.
