Count emissions and mixture starts no longer fail on degenerate inputs (#1136):
- A component the E step empties is held at its parameters by the negative-binomial and beta-binomial M steps (`Reestimate.frozen`, `EmissionMixtureFit.frozen`) rather than refused; a best-of start skips a seeding that raises and names it.
- The NB and BB densities difference Stirling's series above shape 100 (`sal.emissions.rising`): within 7.3e-14 of `mpmath` from 10 to 1e16, where the BB pmf summed to e^3.04 at τ = 1e16. Below 100 they are bitwise unchanged. Both densities are formed once per distinct count and gathered where the parameters are one per state, bitwise: over 10^5 counts at four states the NB takes 5.2 ms and the BB 11.0 ms (9.0 and 22.1 ms before), and above the threshold 5.7 and 12.9 ms.
- `r = inf` is the Poisson, and `NegativeBinomialEmission.from_overdispersion(alpha, mean)` takes `alpha = 0`.
- D² seeding floors a round-off-negative score at zero.
- The HMC, annealing and tempering mixture starts sample the mixture's own likelihood (`emission_objective`, `build_like`) instead of a Gaussian surrogate snapped to a row: at stress they seed at −28,333, −28,682 and −28,794 nats against −29,463, −30,002 and −30,125, the chain at 4.6 s against 9.6 s, its energy and gradient from one compiled pass with the parameter map in closed form. `EmissionMixtureObjective(gradient_on_distinct=True)` takes the gradient on the distinct counts, and the objective keeps those counts across evaluations (`sal.emissions.rising.reusing_distinct`).
- `EmissionMixtureObjective.value_and_gradient` is compiled for the count families without a covariate (`oxisal.count_mixture_value_and_gradient`): every `lgamma` and `digamma` difference a prefix sum over integers, one crossing per call, 1.2 ms against autograd's 3.0 ms at the stress mixture, agreeing to 1.3e-15 in the value and 1.8e-11 in the gradient.
