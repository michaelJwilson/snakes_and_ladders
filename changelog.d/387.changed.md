Eleven copies of the same product enumeration collapse onto one seam in
`snakes_and_ladders.enumeration`, beside the cap of #230: `assignments` and
`assignment_table` build the product, `normalize` shifts and exponentiates,
`accumulate` sums into per-site bins, and `best_assignment` takes the argmax
under the lexicographic tie rule. The three weighted enumerations in
`likelihood`, the three `optimum` copies, the two uncapped `learn`
enumerators, and the torch, parsimony and brute-force tables are adapters
over it; every one is pinned bitwise or value-for-value against the
expression it replaced, and the fifteen committed fixture baselines
recomputed unchanged. The two `learn` enumerators gain the cap they lacked.
The shared table is built from the base-`k` digits of the assignment index
rather than a materialized `itertools.product`: the same array, 2.8x to 6.6x
faster over `3**4` to `2**17`.
