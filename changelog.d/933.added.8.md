`baum_welch_family` runs the E step in the compiled ragged kernel by default, one sequence at a time with no padding: 4.1x the torch recursion on 30 chains of 40-300 positions and 19.4x on 200 chains of 100-3,000, agreeing within 2e-12. `backend=Backend.PYTHON` keeps the torch recursion as the oracle. `fit_transition=False` holds a single transition (R5).
