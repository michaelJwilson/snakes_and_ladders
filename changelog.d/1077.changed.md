Hybrid ground-state solvers are built by one factory (#1077). `opt.compose.Then` chains stages under one budget: each starts from the answer before it, gets what earlier stages left less a reserve, and receives only the options it declares. `search.ground_state.chain(*parts)` builds a `Then` from names or `part(solver, reserve=..., **bound)`. `ground_state` takes a chain by name, e.g. `"field_argmax>descent>alpha-expansion"`; `run_descent` is ICM charged only the sweeps it ran. **Breaking:** `warm_anneal`, `run_swendsen_wang_then_expansion` and `run_expansion_then_swendsen_wang` are removed. The `ARMS` entries are now `chain(...)` expressions, bitwise the functions they replace.
