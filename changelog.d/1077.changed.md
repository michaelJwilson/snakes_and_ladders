Hybrid ground-state solvers are built on the fly (#1077). `opt.compose.Then` chains stages under one budget: each starts from the answer before it, gets what earlier stages left less a reserve, and receives only the options it declares. `opt.compose.BestOf` runs independent realizations from one start, each on an equal share of the budget and its own spawned generator, and keeps the lowest value. `search.ground_state.SolverChain.parse(text)` reads a chain whose stages join by `>`, alternatives by `|`, and repetitions by `x**n` (n realizations of `x`), with parentheses and per-stage arguments, e.g. `"(swendsen-wang(t_start=1.0,reserve_cycles=10)>alpha-expansion)**4"`. `chain.stages[i].update(...)` checks and sets a stage's arguments, and `ground_state` takes the instance or its text. `run_descent` is ICM charged only the sweeps it ran. **Breaking:** `warm_anneal`, `run_swendsen_wang_then_expansion` and `run_expansion_then_swendsen_wang` are removed; the `ARMS` entries are `chain(...)` expressions, bitwise the functions they replace and the text that spells them.
