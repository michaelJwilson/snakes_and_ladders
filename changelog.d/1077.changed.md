Hybrid ground-state solvers are built on the fly by one factory (#1077). `opt.compose.Then` chains stages under one budget: each starts from the answer before it, gets what earlier stages left less a reserve, and receives only the options it declares. `search.ground_state.chain(*parts)` builds a chain from names or `part(solver, reserve=..., **bound)`, and `ground_state` takes either a chain object or its text: parts joined by `>`, each with arguments, e.g. `"swendsen-wang(shape=linear,t_start=0.78,t_end=0.32,reserve_cycles=10)>alpha-expansion"`. The arguments are the schedule's fields and `steps` on an anneal, `backend` and `min_sites` on a descent, and `reserve_cycles` or `reserve_sweeps` on any part. `run_descent` is ICM charged only the sweeps it ran. **Breaking:** `warm_anneal`, `run_swendsen_wang_then_expansion` and `run_expansion_then_swendsen_wang` are removed; the `ARMS` entries are `chain(...)` expressions, bitwise the functions they replace and the text that spells them.
