`oxisal.ragged_posteriors` runs its segments in parallel over `rayon`, GIL released, and takes `threads=None` (the global pool) or a count, as `ising_ground_states` does (#1191). Over 2e5 positions at ten states, 1 thread against 4: 1,222 ms against 320 ms (3.8x), 883 ms against 252 ms under `KRONECKER` (3.5x). The segments are cut into at most 64 blocks by their lengths alone and the transition counts summed within a block, then across blocks in order, so every output is the same, bitwise, at 1, 2, 4 and 8 threads. Against the single-accumulator kernel it replaces, `gamma` and the evidence are unchanged bitwise and the counts move by at most 2.8e-14 relative (declared tolerance 1e-11); a 40-iteration Baum–Welch fit moves by at most 6.4e-13 relative. The Monte-Carlo E step (`ragged.sampled_posteriors`) is kept: at four threads each it reaches within 1 nat of exact EM's 500-iteration log-likelihood in 110 s against 148 s, where #1170 read 107 s against 440 s with the exact kernel on one thread.
