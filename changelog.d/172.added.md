Sum-product belief propagation over a Potts Markov random field (`sal.likelihood.belief_propagation`), returning single-site and pairwise marginals and the Bethe free energy, with two exact oracles beside it in `sal.likelihood.potts`: exhaustive enumeration, and a 2-D strip transfer matrix whose cost is exponential in the strip's width rather than its site count. Belief propagation is exact on a tree and pinned there against enumeration; on a loopy lattice it is approximate and its deviation from the exact strip is reported rather than asserted. Messages that do not converge raise instead of returning a number.
