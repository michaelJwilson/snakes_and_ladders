`sal.search.potts_problem.PottsProblem`: one Potts problem held in Rust (`oxisal.PottsProblem`) across solves, with `argmax`, `icm`, `merge` and `anneal` crossing once each and returning `Labelling`, and `set_field` refreshing the field without rebuilding the graph. `icm` takes `SweepOrder.WORKLIST`, `FloorPolicy`, `FloorAt` and draws the floor's uniforms per sweep in Rust; `merge` takes `MergeGain.HALVED` and `MergePairs.ADJACENT`, the downstream labelling step's rules. Index-order ICM, the draw-free floor, the full merge and the anneal are the existing calls' labellings bitwise; a site whose neighbourhood has not changed is skipped, exactly, which takes ICM on the downstream package's shipped easy instance from 1.73 ms to 0.27 ms.
