Annealing schedules as parameters (#1038): `sample.schedule.ScheduleParams` (shape exponential, linear or cosine, both endpoints, a held fraction) builds a `TempSchedule` of any length. `search.ground_state.run_annealed` takes a schedule, a fixed step count and a start, defaulting to the previous run bitwise; `anneal_potts` takes an `initial` labelling; `warm_anneal` anneals ICM's labelling. `qa.potts_schedule` tunes Swendsen-Wang's and Wolff's schedules on seeds 5 to 9 into `docs/nb/data/potts_schedule.json`, and `potts_starts.ipynb` reports them on seeds 0 to 4.
