`sal.opt.hmm.forward_log_likelihood_ragged` takes keyword-only `switch` and `switch_kind` (#1186): the differentiable forward over segments with a per-position switch, under every `SwitchKind`, each step taken in its factors. It equals the summed evidence of `sal.likelihood.ragged.posteriors` within 1e-12 and its score gradient is that kernel's posterior within 1e-12. `SwitchKind` is defined in `sal.opt.hmm` and re-exported from `sal.likelihood.ragged`. Forward plus backward over 200 segments of 100-3,000 positions takes 2.37 s at K = 16, against 53.34 s for a dense per-step kernel.
