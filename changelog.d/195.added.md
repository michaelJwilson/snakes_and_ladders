`sal.learn` carries two more instances of `Environment`: the Potts landscape over an arbitrary graph, via `PottsLandscape.on_graph`, and `sal.learn.hmm.StatePathLandscape`, a search over hidden state paths at known parameters. Both are pinned against the enumerated estimator oracle in `sal.learn.exact` and against exhaustive enumeration of their own state spaces, and neither takes an application type — the no-application-imports rule holds, so a caller unpacks a `PottsGraph` or an `HmmParams` into plain arrays.
