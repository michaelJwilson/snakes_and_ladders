`EmissionFamily.reestimate` and `EmissionFamily.sample` take NumPy arrays as well as tensors for `observations`, `posterior` and `covariate` (#1011): neither takes a derivative, so a caller holding arrays no longer builds a tensor to call them. Each family converts at entry through the new `emissions.base.as_tensor`, reading the observations in its `observation_dtype`; a tensor passes through uncast, so every existing call is unchanged, and the widening is backward-compatible. `log_density` stays tensor in, tensor out, since the objectives differentiate through it; families still hold tensor parameters, so `emissions` still imports torch. `search.spatio_sequential.m_step` hands its blocks over as arrays and the module no longer imports torch itself, reading the covariate through the new `likelihood.spatio_sequential.covariate_columns`, the array slice `covariate_block` now wraps. `sim.count_pairs.ReflectedEmission.reestimate` does its reshape and reflection in NumPy, `IndependentCountPair` splits its covariate as an array, and `vertex_covariate` returns an `np.ndarray` where it returned a tensor. On seeded inputs over 25 family configurations and both M-step backends, 220 of 220 recorded outputs of `sample` and `reestimate` called with arrays equal the tensor calls before the change byte for byte, and 261 of 261 outputs with tensors, including the 64-vertex count-pair draw and four coupled M steps, equal them too.
