`merge_small_labels(graph, field, labelling, rng, *, min_sites)` floors a labelling from any solver, ICM's floor from that labelling, and `merge(min_sites=k)` is its chain stage, e.g. `alpha-expansion>merge(min_sites=20)`. `ground_state` no longer forwards its default `backend` and `min_sites` over a stage's own, so `icm(min_sites=k)` runs its floor, which it silently dropped before (#1081).
