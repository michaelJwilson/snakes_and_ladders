`search.infer` fans one neighbourhood's candidate fits across cores through
`snakes_and_ladders.parallel`, as `workers`, `backend` and `intra_op_threads`.
The candidates are independent and their results are combined in input order,
so a parallel search returns the serial search bitwise: 58.87 s to 21.67 s at
50 taxa on 4 processes (2.72x), at an identical log-likelihood and topology.
Neither argument has a parallel default, because which backend pays inverts
with the problem: threads win 1.20x at 8 taxa and 20,000 sites, where the work
is inside `torch` kernels, and lose 1.99x at 20 taxa and 2,000 sites, where it
is Python in the post-order.
