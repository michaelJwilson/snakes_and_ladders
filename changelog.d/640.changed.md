The textbook's applicability tables are derived from the suite rather than
read from `PROBLEMS.md`'s inventory columns (#640). A method applies to a
problem when a test of either significant kind names a symbol of the method
and a fixture of the problem's key; the *claim* that a family applies stays
hand-written in `docs/tex/method_notes.yaml`, so the check is not circular,
and a family the suite runs that no note claims now fails the generation. Two
consequences: the untested pairings fall from 13 to 9, four of them having
been listed only because the reading missed their tests; and the count-pair
coupled row reaches no oracle, its claimed one having been a catalogue cell no
test backed (#646).
