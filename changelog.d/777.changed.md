`sal.sample` is where a sampler lives: eleven modules moved there from `opt` and `search` --- the continuous samplers (`hmc`, `langevin`, `slice`, `schedule`), the discrete ones (`potts_mcmc`, `gibbs`, `balanced`, `potts_keyed`), tempering and annealing (`tempered`, `annealed`) and the diagnostics (`statistics`). An optimizer is judged by the optimum it reaches and a sampler by the distribution it converges to, and the rules for the two were stated in two files; `sample/CLAUDE.md` now holds the sampler rules and each donor keeps only what is its own. `from sal.opt import hmc` is now `from sal.sample import hmc`, and the 83 files that named a donor were repointed in this change. No behaviour changed --- no module carried a relative import, so the moved files are byte-identical apart from their path, and every referee ran from the new location.
