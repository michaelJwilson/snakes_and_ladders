`sal.learn.rollout.rollout` takes `stop_at_local_optimum`, and `sal.learn.policy` gains `EpsilonGreedyPolicy` and the narrower `Policy` protocol it satisfies. Together they let an episode continue past a state no action improves and take the worsening move that leaves it — measured on the issue #177 fixture, where escape from a local optimum rises from 0.111 at epsilon 0 to 0.883 at 0.4. Both default to the previous behaviour.
