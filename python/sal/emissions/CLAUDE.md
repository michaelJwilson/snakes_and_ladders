# CLAUDE.md --- `sal.emissions`

The root `CLAUDE.md` governs; this file holds the module's principles only.

*   **One construction per density.** The beta-binomial is `log C`, the two log rates and the scaled rising factorials `S(x, m) = R(x, m) - m log x` from `rising.scaled_rising_array` and its table, `rising.scaled_rising_table` (#1341), summed in the order `bb` states, on every NumPy and compiled route: `bb.beta_binomial_log_pmf`, `bb.trial_tables` and the kernels that read them agree bit for bit. The torch `log_density` keeps its own arithmetic and is held to a declared tolerance, not re-recorded (#1332).
*   **No difference of two large `lgamma`.** A term of size `x log x` differenced to a value of size `m log x` is written as a scaled rising factorial, and the `m log x` parts are collected into logarithms of rates.
*   **The negative binomial is built the same way.** `nb`'s table is `S(r, y) - lgamma(y + 1)` and its exposure term `(T + y log(lambda / (1 + q))) - r log1p(q)`, in the one order `nb` states, on every NumPy and compiled route; `nb.negative_binomial_log_pmf` is the reference and `r = inf` its Poisson (#1335). A score that differences two `digamma` at one large argument is `rising.digamma_rising`.
*   **Code integers, never covariates.** `coded.encode` codes a count per distinct `(label, count)`, `label` one integer array per observation that the caller builds (e.g. `x + Z·y`), never a tuple; the continuous covariate stays per observation and its term is completed after the lookup, through the kernel `emissions.dense` reads (#1340). A table spans the distinct counts, not every count to the largest.
