How much alignment the search needs to recover the tree that generated it. Each marker is one independent alignment drawn from the 4-state Jukes-Cantor fixture (seed 20260905, 8 replicates at each of 6 site counts), inferred by NNI hill climbing and scored against the generating topology by normalized Robinson-Foulds distance -- 0 for the same tree, 1 for no internal split in common. The dotted line is the accuracy requirement this project set itself, 0.05. The mean over replicates meets the requirement at the longest alignment swept, 2000 sites, and misses it at the shortest, 60. The distance is normalized by internal splits, so the bound means the same thing at any taxon count.
