The waterfall of the memory-2 (7, 5) turbo code at K = 256: rate 0.330 unpunctured, both constituent encoders terminated, a uniformly random interleaver drawn under seed 233, on the binary-input Gaussian channel. (a) Bit error rate at 1, 2, 4 and 8 iterations of the extrinsic exchange, each point 50 frames and 12\_800 message bits, with the one-sigma binomial interval that sample supports; the dashed line is the uncoded antipodal closed form Q(sqrt(2 Eb/N0)), which the coded curve falls below at every point, reaching below 8e-05 at 2 dB against 0.038 uncoded. (b) Frame error rate at 8 iterations on the same axis. A point at which no frame erred is drawn at one over the sample size with an arrow: the sample bounds the rate from above and says nothing below it. The interval understates the spread of the estimate, since bit errors inside one frame are correlated; it is the bar the sample size supports if the bits were independent, and a floor on the true one.
