The three continuous test functions in two dimensions, with the minimizers known in closed form and the endpoints of L-BFGS fits from Gaussian random restarts around each function's own start, the origin for Rastrigin (seed 20260908). (a) Rosenbrock, 4 restarts at scale 1: 4 of 4 reach the minimizer (1, 1). (b) Rastrigin, 16 restarts at scale 2: 1 of 16 reach the global minimum at the origin, every fit stopping at the local minimum of the cell it started in, so a single fit of this surface reports convergence and not optimality. (c) Himmelblau, 8 restarts at scale 3: 8 of 8 reach one of the four equal minima, which one depending on the basin the start fell in. Contours are drawn in log(1 + value).
