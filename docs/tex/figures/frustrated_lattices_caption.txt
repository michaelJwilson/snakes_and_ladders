Two frustrated instances with an answer from outside. (a) The 3x3 periodic triangular Ising antiferromagnet, coupling -1, 27 bonds (dotted: bonds wrapping the torus), with one of its enumerated ground states coloured by spin. Heavy bonds join agreeing spins: 9 of 27, one in three, which the double count over triangles fixes as the minimum at every size, so the ground-state energy is 9 exactly. (b) A planted Viana-Bray spin glass on 60 sites at mean degree 4, couplings of magnitude 1, one instance per frustration from seed 20260908: the energy of the planted state against the lowest energy reached by 20 single-site descents from random starts. Descent lands on the planted energy at 3 of 8 frustrations and goes below it at 5, where the planted state is an upper bound on the ground state and not the ground state.
