The Tanner graph of one draw from the regular (3, 6) Gallager ensemble: the enumerable instance, 12 bits under seed 340, decoded on a binary symmetric channel at crossover 0.15. (a) Bits as circles, the 6 parity checks as squares, and one edge per nonzero of the parity-check matrix: 36 of them, every bit on 3 checks and every check on 6 bits. (b) The same nonzeros as the matrix, its 3 bands of 2 rows separated by a rule. The first band is drawn heavy in both panels: its checks cover consecutive bits by construction, and every later band is that band under a uniform random permutation of the columns, which is the whole of the randomness in the draw. The construction fixes the degrees at every block length, so the longer instances are this structure at a size no page holds; this one is drawn because a page holds it.
