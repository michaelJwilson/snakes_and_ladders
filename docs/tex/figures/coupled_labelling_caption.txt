The coupled model's labelling on the instance past enumeration: a 10x10 open lattice, 2 classes, 2 chain states, 4 positions, spatial prior at inverse temperature 1 and coupling 1, observations drawn from seed 50 under the planted labelling and fitted from seed 0. (a) The planting: half the columns in each class, so the boundary is one cut of 10 bonds. (b) The labelling block ascent reaches from the annealed start, permuted onto the planted classes and ringed at every node where the two panels differ: 0 of 100 nodes are labelled otherwise, an agreement of 1.00. (c) The external field at the fit, as the cost of the first class less that of the second, with the sign carrying which class the observations at that node favour on their own. Taking each node at its cheaper class, with the spatial prior switched off, misses the planting at 15 of 100 nodes, ringed: at 4 positions the emissions overlap too far to label a node one at a time, and it is the prior that recovers them.
