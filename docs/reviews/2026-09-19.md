# Review, 2026-09-19

**TL;DR.** Of the nine rows issue #755 puts to root `CLAUDE.md`'s rule --- one
abstraction where it aligns and simplifies several use cases --- **one meets it
and is folded here**, two are already the seam and want a guard, five are left
as they are, and one is not an abstraction question at all. The fold is the
composition issue #387 left behind: enumerate, score, take the first maximizer,
written in full by `learn.potts.optimum`, `learn.hmm.optimum` and
`learn.relaxed.enumerate_optimum`, now `enumeration.enumerated_optimum` and
three calls, bitwise. Three of the ticket's own figures were impressions this
audit corrects: `SimulatedDataset` is not the base of the `suffix:Dataset`
five, the `prefix:Exact` five have zero *package* consumers and are each a
module-local function's return type, and the 15 findings outside every cluster
are **10 the survey misreads** and 5 already decided. Two findings are filed:
`likelihood.parsimony.brute_force_parsimony_score` builds the product space
with its own `itertools.product` and no cap, and
`infra/appraise_structures.py` reports a decided store as a recompute in three
ways.

The tree read is `main` at `048a342` and the branch `claude/infra-755-abstractions`
from it. Every count below is the output of `infra/appraise_structures.py` and

### 1.2 The seam that landed

`snakes_and_ladders.enumeration.enumerated_optimum(n_states, n_sites, score, *,
what, limit)`, owned by `enumeration.py`, which already owns `configurations`,
`posterior`, `site_marginals` and `argmax`. The implementation that stays as
the oracle is `learn.relaxed.enumerate_optimum`'s body, moved without
reordering: build the candidates from `configurations`, one `score` call per
candidate in enumeration order, `argmax`, return `(candidate, float(value))`.

The callers that become calls are `learn.potts.optimum`,
`learn.hmm.optimum` and `learn.relaxed.enumerate_optimum`. Each keeps its
signature, its return type and its tie rule; what each supplies is which sizes
and which score. `enumeration.py`'s docstring already recorded the fold's other
half --- "three took the argmax over it with their own loop" --- and left the
composition around it written three times.

`likelihood.hmm_paths` is the one call of `enumeration.argmax` outside the
module and is not folded: it reads the score vector again to build the
posterior, so the scores outlive the argmax and a function returning one score
cannot carry it. `tests/regression/test_duplication_guards.py` names it, so a
fourth copy fails a test.

**The referees.** `tests/regression/test_enumeration_seam.py` carries the
deleted body verbatim and runs it beside the seam at (2,1), (2,6), (3,4) and
(5,3) on a seeded unstructured score; the comparison is `==` on both the
configuration and the score, and it passes --- the fold reorders no arithmetic,
so bitwise is what it costs and no tolerance is declared. The pins carried
across the fold and run before the old bodies went:
`learn/test_learn_potts.py::test_the_optimum_is_the_best_of_every_configuration`,
`learn/test_learn_hmm.py::test_hill_climbing_reaches_the_enumerated_optimum`,
the three `enumerate_optimum` assertions in `learn/test_learn_relaxed.py`, and
`test_enumeration_seam.py`'s own product-space and argmax tests. 70 tests, all
passing, none loosened.

### 1.3 The enumerate-shaped functions

19, over spaces that are not each other's: topologies (`(2n-5)!!`, which
`configurations` cannot express), cuts, codewords, messages, hidden paths,
mixture assignments, ancestral labellings and spin configurations. Fifteen of
the sixteen modules already import `snakes_and_ladders.enumeration`;
`search.surrogate` reaches it through `enumerate_potts` and `search.topology`
enumerates trees. **One does not and should:**
`likelihood.parsimony.brute_force_parsimony_score` builds
`itertools.product(range(k), repeat=len(internal))` itself, with no cap ---
the sixth copy #387 counted five of, and the one unit `enumeration.py`'s
docstring names (`k ** internal` ancestral labellings) that never arrived. It
is filed rather than fixed here: adding the cap adds a refusal where there is
none, which is a behaviour change this pull request excludes.

### 1.4 Outside every cluster: recompute or store

The survey prints 15 findings on classes in no cluster --- 10 "derives a
compressed layout per call", 5 "carries a list of lists". Read at the source,
**every one of the 10 is decided already and the survey cannot see the
decision**, in three ways:

- **The word, not the layout** (2). `emissions.NegativeBinomialEmission.reestimate`'s
  `offsets` is the negative-binomial exposure offset and
  `learn.potts_nd.PottsNDEnvironment._sited_candidates`' is
  `np.tile(np.arange(1, n_states), n_nodes)`, a label rotation. Neither
  addresses a relation. This is the `restarts`-for-`starts` failure the survey
  was written to avoid, one spelling later.
- **A constructor** (4). `search.potts_keyed.SwendsenWangMove.__init__`,
  `WolffMove.__init__`, `learn.surrogate.Examples.__post_init__` and
  `_Batch.__init__` each build the layout once per object. The survey excludes
  a `classmethod` constructor on exactly this reasoning (#642) and does not
  exclude `__init__`.
- **A store it cannot read** (4). `search.gibbs._Indexed.layout` memoizes by
  hand into `self._layout`, which the `cached_property`/`lru_cache` test
  misses; `_Indexed.log_density`, `SwendsenWangMove.propose` and
  `WolffMove.propose` read `self._offsets`, where the rule looks for
  `self.offsets`. `WolffMove.__init__` carries the decision in a comment:
  "Built once and held ... recompute or store, decided as store".

The 5 list-of-lists findings are decided: `likelihood.schedule.Layout` was
measured and declined (the whole layout is 0.6 per cent of a `sum_product` over
a 2,000-variable chain, #592); `sandbox.pruning_burn._Flattened` sits in
`sandbox/`, which is declined work; `learn.potts.PottsEnvironment` and
`learn.potts_nd.PottsNDEnvironment` keep their own adjacency because `learn/`
imports no application module, which
`tests/regression/test_duplication_guards.py` already records; and
`search.gibbs._Indexed.touching` holds `(Factor, axis, columns)` triples, which
offsets into one array cannot hold --- the compiled sweep reads `_EdgeLayout`,
which is `csr`. The pricing of what remains is #690's, and root `CLAUDE.md`'s
default with no profile is recompute.
