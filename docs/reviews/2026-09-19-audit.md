# Audit, 2026-09-19

**TL;DR.** Read whole against what it declares, the repository holds its
numbers and not its rules: of the **206 rules** the ten `CLAUDE.md` files
state, a test enforces 146 and **60 are checked by nobody**, among them the
two port bars the whole backend policy rests on; **one rule cites a figure
`STATUS.md` contradicts** (root `CLAUDE.md:67`'s 6.3× for #289), **two pairs

request, four in PR #789, and four tickets carry the rest (#790 to #793).
