# Design audit, 2026-09-20

**TL;DR:** read by hand on `main` at `89b8bc4`, with the open stack #837--#851
read for what each lands: the tree carries **17 named patterns**, complete in
**4** of their applications and half-applied in the rest; of DRY and the five
CLEAN properties, **non-redundancy is the one broken at cost** --- the same
arithmetic is written **12 times** for a Metropolis accept, **7** for a
forward--backward pass, **5** for a rescaled pruning step, **3** for iterated
conditional modes, **3** for an expectation--maximization loop, **9** for a
Markdown table reader and **54** for the repository root --- with
encapsulation second (**8** private names imported across **4** module pairs in
`sample/`, module-level caches keyed on `id()`, a result typed `Any`) and loose
coupling third (one declared cycle, an oracle that imports its referent, four
function-local imports that hide twin cycles). Cohesion and assertiveness hold
at the package level and fail at the seam: one `Budget` counts in **8**
spellings, "converged" has **5** encodings, and `Backend` names **3** different
axes. **30 refactors** are tabled at the end, **26** of them referential; the
4 that are not are correctness findings and say so.

## What this audit reads, and how

Package by package, by the model: each of `sim/`, `likelihood/`, `search/` +
`opt/`, `sample/` + `learn/`, and `qa/` + `infra/` + the root + the guard tests
was read in full for the patterns it carries, the intent it writes twice, the
concept it spells two ways, and the pattern it applies to some members and not
others. The per-package reads were taken at `73e4d1b`; the merges of #835,
#838, #841 and #843 landed while they ran, and every finding those four touch
--- the `Simulator` seam, the `Params` protocol, the ladder's `Cost`, the
tracker's reach --- was re-read at `89b8bc4` and is stated as it stands there.
Cross-cutting counts are taken once at the root and carry the command that
reproduces them, per #611's plan: no survey script, nothing to keep in step.

The constraint is the calling review's, with one clause added: a fold never
changes what a call returns; an oracle is a rung and a rung is never merged;
an oracle gains no code shared with the route it referees, so a shared core
serves the routes and never the oracle (R3); and a refactor is proposed with
the referee that would let its old path go. Overlap between two rungs is
recorded, not folded: the ladder is the evidence, and a shared helper below
a rung is plumbing only where neither rung is pinned against the other. Where a finding is a
defect rather than a duplication, the table says so and the row is a bug
ticket, not a fold.

Prior work this builds on and does not restate: #230 (three duplications given
one home, the duplication guard), #400 and #403 (the seams inventory), #611
(the pattern plan; its non-goal --- no hand-written pattern catalogue --- is
kept, so the table below names members by symbol and is refereed by the
`api_map` guard that resolves them), the 2026-09-19 seams review (16 seams,
consumer counts) and the 2026-09-20 calling review (five spellings per intent,
six forbidden edges, six folds queued, of which #834, #835, #836, #838 and
#849 have landed or are open).

## The principles, graded

CLEAN is read as **c**ohesive, **l**oosely coupled, **e**ncapsulated,
**a**ssertive, **n**on-redundant; DRY is the last letter stated at the level of
a line rather than an abstraction. Ordered by what each breach costs.

**1. Non-redundant / DRY --- broken, and the cost is lines and drift.** The same
computation written out `n` times is `n - 1` places a fix does not reach, and
the copies here have already drifted:

- A Metropolis accept (`log u < Δ`) is written **12** ways
  (`sample/potts_mcmc.py:804, 1449, 1923, 2087`, `sample/gibbs.py:651, 969`,
  `sample/annealed.py:602`, `sample/tempered.py:330`, `sample/hmc.py:962,
  1077`, `sample/langevin.py:265`, `learn/potts_nd.py:553`); one is negated,
  one carries `β = ∞`, two use `math.exp` where the rest use `np.exp`, one has
  a NaN guard the others lack. Only `_niedermayer_accept` is a named function.
- A forward pass over a chain is written **7** times
  (`likelihood/forward_backward.py:125` and again at `:172` inside
  `sample_path`, `opt/hmm.py:951`, `likelihood/convolutional.py:181`,
  `likelihood/ragged_rust.py:26` and `:66`, `spatio_sequential_rust.py:189`);
  `step_kernels`' constant-or-varying dispatch is written twice with the same
  error text (`forward_backward.py:43`, `opt/hmm.py:1000`).
- The rescale-and-accumulate step of Felsenstein pruning is written **4**
  times and omitted a fifth (`likelihood/pruning.py:112`,
  `pruning_torch.py:386` and `:530`, `pruning_analytic.py:194`,
  `surrogate.py:169` with no rescaling); the leaf one-hot **5** times; the
  `pi`-shape, missing-leaf and branch-length validations **6**, **5** and **3**
  times with identical messages; `_postorder` twice byte-identical
  (`pruning_rust.py:51`, `pruning_analytic.py:89`) and imported privately a

- Iterated conditional modes is written **3** times
  (`search/alpha_expansion.py:391`, `search/ground_state.py:489`,
  `search/spatio_sequential.py:244`); the second's docstring says the copies

**2. Encapsulated --- broken where modules reach for each other's privates.**
`sample/potts_keyed.py:40` imports six underscore names from
`sample.potts_mcmc`; `sample/tempered.py:39-49` imports five from

**3. Loosely coupled --- one cycle declared, three hidden.** The
`sample <-> search` cycle is the one edge #830 could not cut and is declared

cycle behind a function-local import: `convolutional.py:172`,
`message_passing.py:287`, `search/maxflow.py:416`, `sim/count_pairs.py:713`

**4. Assertive --- the objects do not own their vocabulary.** `Budget.unit` is

(`"evaluations"`, `"decisions"`, `"passes"`, `"sweeps"`, `"fits"`,
`"site-visits"`, `"site visits"`, `"candidates"`), while `infra/ladder.py:52`
already declares the `Cost` enum #841 landed for the same axis. "Did it finish"
is a bool on 4 results, a raise on 2, a caught exception on 1, an iteration
count the caller compares on 4, a derived property on 1, and nothing on 6.
`theta_from_truth` exists on 11 objectives with 10 signatures and is on the
`Objective` protocol for none, so `tests/regression/opt/test_opt_intervals.py:90-180`
hand-writes 91 lines of per-class construction. `TestFunctionMetrics.__call__:233`
branches on `isinstance(self.objective, Himmelblau)` where a `minimizer`
method exists on the other two. `capacity.py:173` and `:232` are two
`isinstance`/`is` chains over the three channels the `Channel` protocol was
declared to abstract.

**5. Cohesive --- holds per directory, fails per module.** The calling review's
placement findings stand: `opt.budget` and `search.ground_state` are study
harnesses, `likelihood.turbo.measure_error_rates`,
`likelihood.css.measure_logical_error_rate` and `likelihood.ldpc.erasure_threshold`
are measurements, `likelihood.features` is `learn/`'s, `search.support` is
statistics, `sim` holds five decoders. Added here: `emissions.py` is **2,739**
lines, the largest module by 3x; `sim/fixtures.py` spends 330 of 695 lines
(48%) on a baseline apparatus that serves 5 records over 3 of 20 problems;
`search/infer.py:362 infer` takes 15 parameters and reaches 2² x 3 search
strategies through them; `sample/kernels.py:86 gibbs_sweep_sites` takes 14.
